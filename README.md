## Continuity: read this first
Established 2026-11-03. The objective is to identify **investments worth acting on now AND future candidates**, with disciplined QARP, measured risk, dated decisions and prospective outcomes. Research must produce **actionable BUY STARTER / BUY FULL / WAIT / REJECT judgments**, not perpetual analysis. This public repository is research: it contains no actual purchases, private positions, account balances or automatic trading.

The workflow is **Sector → Industry → Company quality → Valuation → Momentum → Earnings/event gates → Research decision → Review**. A great business, a reasonable price and a suitable entry condition must each be established separately.

The latest recovered baseline is **Discovery v2**, not the earlier stricter QARP screen. Use [Finviz settings](docs/finviz-filters.md). The three permanent research lanes are one emerging rotation sector and two established rotation sectors; sector membership is reviewed weekly rather than fixed forever.

## Three complementary investment mandates — STRATEGY-001
The adopted investment strategy has **three complementary outcomes**: **(1) strategic long-horizon compounders** (5–10-year business ownership thesis and staged wealth-management assessment); **(2) immediate measured-risk opportunities** (priced conditional buy/wait/reject decisions); and **(3) future candidates** (explicit price/event/fundamental triggers). **Long-term thesis classification and current entry readiness are separate**: a strategic compounder can be BUY/WAIT depending on valuation and risk. Neither technology-sector fear nor temporary weak momentum is by itself a veto on an exceptional long-term business.
