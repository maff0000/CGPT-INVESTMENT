# CGPT-INVESTMENT
Public, dated research journal for **quality at a reasonable price (QARP)** with improving sector and stock momentum.

## Continuity: read this first
Established 2026-11-03. The objective is to develop a repeatable research process, record decisions and outcomes, and eventually transfer the proven process into an application. This repository contains research candidates, not investment approvals, portfolio holdings or a trading system.

The workflow is **Sector → Industry → Company quality → Valuation → Momentum → Earnings/event gates → Research decision → Review**. A great business, a reasonable price and a suitable entry condition must each be established separately.

The latest recovered baseline is **Discovery v2**, not the earlier stricter QARP screen. Use [Finviz settings](docs/finviz-filters.md). The three permanent research lanes are one emerging rotation sector and two established rotation sectors; sector membership is reviewed weekly rather than fixed forever.

| Initial lane | Sector | Original research candidates |
|---|---|---|
| Emerging rotation | Industrials | ZWS, EME, FERG |
| Established rotation A | Healthcare | ALGN, GMED, ADMA |
| Established rotation B | Consumer Defensive | MDLZ, PEP, OLLI |

These sector roles and company theses are **PROVISIONAL**. The original nine are a starting shortlist, not nine confirmed screen passes. Near-misses and recovery candidates must be labelled. Aim for 3–5 research candidates per lane and roughly 10–15 weekly reviews, allowing fewer when quality is insufficient. The initial nine do not imply that the weekly target has been met. Do not force new names or purchases.

## Current state and evidence limits
- Methodology and original research list were recovered from the founding discussion. Selected observations are retained as **REPORTED**, not independently verified market facts.
- Historical Finviz counts, ratios, sector returns and earnings dates have not been rerun or independently confirmed in this initialisation.
- No candidate has a completed fair-value range, approved entry price, verified event gate or approved investment status.
- The exact earnings exclusion window was not supplied. The conservative draft in [earnings gates](docs/earnings-risk-gates.md) is **PROPOSED**, pending confirmation.
- No automated monitoring, scheduled jobs, market-data feeds or application have been deployed.

## Resume the work
1. Read this README, AGENTS, the latest decisions and latest review.
2. Refresh sector and industry returns, relative strength and breadth at one common observation time.
3. Verify each company's identity, exchange, latest filing, material events and next earnings announcement using public primary sources.
4. Re-run A/B/C screens and record exact filters, timestamps, counts and near-miss exceptions. Do not assume the historical shortlist still qualifies.
5. Begin detailed company research with ZWS, GMED and MDLZ, one from each lane; an earnings block does not prevent desk research.
6. Establish fair-value scenarios, downside, invalidation evidence and a review trigger. Retain UNKNOWN fields until supported.
7. Publish a dated review, decision changes and corrections. Keep public hypothetical outcomes separate from any private investing activity.
