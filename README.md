# CGPT-INVESTMENT
Public, dated research journal for **quality at a reasonable price (QARP)** with improving sector and stock momentum.

## Continuity: read this first
Established 2026-11-03. The objective is to identify **investments worth acting on now AND future candidates**, with disciplined QARP, measured risk, dated decisions and prospective outcomes. Research must produce **actionable BUY STARTER / BUY FULL / WAIT / REJECT judgments**, not perpetual analysis. This public repository is research: it contains no actual purchases, private positions, account balances or automatic trading.

The workflow is **Sector → Industry → Company quality → Valuation → Momentum → Earnings/event gates → Research decision → Review**. A great business, a reasonable price and a suitable entry condition must each be established separately.

The latest recovered baseline is **Discovery v2**, not the earlier stricter QARP screen. Use [Finviz settings](docs/finviz-filters.md). The three permanent research lanes are one emerging rotation sector and two established rotation sectors; sector membership is reviewed weekly rather than fixed forever.

These sector roles and company theses are **PROVISIONAL**. The original nine are a starting shortlist, not nine confirmed screen passes. Near-misses and recovery candidates must be labelled. Aim for 3–5 research candidates per lane and roughly 10–15 weekly reviews, allowing fewer when quality is insufficient. The initial nine do not imply that the weekly target has been met. Do not force new names or purchases.

## Two decision queues — active from 2026-11-03
- **NOW:** decision-ready priority review, with current price, earnings gate, valuation/risk scenarios, and a concise **buy / wait / reject** outcome. A small *starter* may be appropriate despite bounded uncertainty; that is different from gambling. See [decision-to-invest](docs/decision-to-invest.md).
- **LATER:** quality or recovering names with a specific entry price, earnings result, catalyst or confirmation event to watch. The weekly three-sector structure is a **discovery funnel**, not a ban on acting.
- Initial ranked [NOW/LATER queue](research/watchlists/2026-11-03-investment-priority.md): **ALGN**, **EME**, **MDLZ** receive immediate decision attention. The remaining six names stay monitored; this is a *research* queue, not an executed order.

## Current state and evidence limits
- Methodology and original research list were recovered from the founding discussion. Selected observations are retained as **REPORTED**, not independently verified market facts.
- Historical Finviz counts, ratios, sector returns and earnings dates have not been rerun or independently confirmed in this initialisation.
- No candidate has a final broker-ready entry decision; **ALGN is the first priority for a near-term starter-position assessment**, and EME/MDLZ have explicit WAIT verdicts at the observed snapshots. See [decision desk](research/reviews/2026-11-03-investment-decision-desk.md).
- **Earnings gate adopted for the manual pilot:** no new entry during the **seven exchange trading sessions before earnings**, including announcement day; require **two complete subsequent regular sessions** and fresh analysis after release. See [earnings gates](docs/earnings-risk-gates.md) and the [dated correction](research/decisions/2026-11-03-earnings-gate-adoption.md).
- No automated monitoring, scheduled jobs, market-data feeds or application have been deployed.

## Resume the work
1. Read this README, AGENTS, the latest decisions and latest review.
2. Refresh sector and industry returns, relative strength and breadth at one common observation time.
3. Verify each company's identity, exchange, latest filing, material events and next earnings announcement using public primary sources.
4. Re-run A/B/C screens and record exact filters, timestamps, counts and near-miss exceptions. Do not assume the historical shortlist still qualifies.
5. Read the [first verified research cycle](research/reviews/2026-11-03-cycle-01.md), [subsequent earnings event verification](research/reviews/2026-11-03-events-02.md) and [initial company dossiers](research/companies/). Continue valuation, momentum and company event checks; an earnings block does not prevent desk research.
6. **Produce an immediate buy / wait / reject verdict** for the top NOW candidate using [investment-decision template](templates/investment-decision.md). Establish defensible abbreviated fair-value/downside scenarios and a meaningful condition for any WAIT, rather than waiting for exhaustive research.
7. Publish a dated review, decision changes and corrections. Keep public hypothetical outcomes separate from any private investing activity.
