# ACTION-001 — Immediate decisions AND future candidates
Date: 2026-11-03 (Europe/London)
Status: **ADOPTED operating objective** (owner correction); not a transaction authority.

## Problem
The original public journal correctly emphasised evidence and patient decision-making but unintentionally encouraged a never-ending research loop. The user specifically clarified: **seek immediate investments as well as future candidates**, accepting measured and managed investment risk rather than confusing it with gambling.

## Decision
Introduce two explicit queues: **NOW** for near-term investments with a price-aware BUY STARTER / BUY FULL / WAIT / REJECT opinion, **LATER** for watchlist names with precise promotion triggers. A weekly review that offers no investment decision (even a defensible WAIT) is incomplete.

Allow a bounded starter-tranche assessment with a concise risk/valuation case. Do **not** require a comprehensive institution-grade model as a prerequisite for every decision. Do **not** relax hard earnings/event verification or ignore material unknowns. Position risk must be sized to credible downside, correlated exposures and the capital allocation chosen privately by the investor.

## This replaces
An interpretation that the programme is purely research and can never reach a purchase decision. It does not authorise the assistant to place trades or publish personal portfolio information. No buy is mandatory and no threshold is an evidence-free certainty claim.

## Initial implication
Promote ALGN, MDLZ, EME to **NOW decision review**, with transparent competing risk/reward evidence. Keep the other six original names in the LATER/discovery queue unless subsequent reviews justify promotion. The original nine are preserved; these three are a subset, not additions. ALGN is the first *starter-review* candidate, not an executed purchase or automatic entry.
