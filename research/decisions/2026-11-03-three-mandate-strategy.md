# STRATEGY-001 — Three complementary investment mandates
**Adopted:** 2026-11-03, Europe/London. **Status:** owner-confirmed strategic direction. Supersedes only interpretations that all purchases must be tactical/rotation trades; preserves historic watchlists and decisions.

## Decision
Document the investment programme as **three complementary outcomes**:

1. **Strategic compounders** — long-term potential ownership of exceptional businesses, working assessment horizon **5–10 years**, with disciplined valuation and staged acquisition for wealth-management consideration. The owner has nominated Alphabet (GOOGL) as an initial example; previous [STRATEGIC-001](2026-11-03-alphabet-long-term.md) is preserved.
2. **Immediate measured-risk opportunities** — companies where valuation and current circumstances can justify conditional purchases now, with evidence-based downside, position limits and event gates, e.g. dated ALGN/EME starter assessments.
3. **Future candidates** — businesses that could qualify at better prices, after events or once operational evidence improves, with **specific promotion triggers**, never indefinite unlabeled monitoring.

**Important design choice:** strategy horizon and *entry state* are **orthogonal**. An Alphabet-type strategic candidate may be **NOW for staged accumulation** or **WAIT for a better price**, without changing its long-term classification. No holding becomes good merely because it is strategic.

## Why
A single technical rotation lens can miss durable compounding businesses; a pure long-term-quality lens can ignore excellent timely purchase opportunities; an endless watchlist can prevent investing. The combined model seeks **ownership, value, risk control and learning**, not output for output's sake. AI-sector fear neither disqualifies all Technology names nor justifies ignoring valuation or AI capex risk.
