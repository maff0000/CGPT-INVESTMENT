# CHART-001 — Adopt chart-stage classification and sector context
**Date:** 2026-11-05 Europe/London. **Decision:** adopted operating pilot, pending real OHLCV chart audit.

Retain the exact Finviz QARP v2 A/B/C screens. Add a second, orthogonal chart-stage classification:
- **BREAKING OUT:** verified close above a documented base/resistance, high priority for immediate decision review.
- **IN TREND:** established positive trend, possibly still investable on a rational continuation/pullback.
- **WATCH:** attractive research candidate without a validated near-term chart entry; specify promotion condition.

**CHART PENDING** and **INVALIDATED** are workflow states, not bullish groups. Every chart record must include actual **sector/industry, sector and stock strength versus benchmark, SMA50/SMA200, breakout/trigger level and volume evidence**, plus an independent **valuation/earnings-based action**. Breaking out does not overrule risk or justify a forced BUY.

Pilot look-up priority: B/C breakout candidates plus recovering A names; in-trend follow-through; A and exceptions for Watch. Optional Finviz chart Pattern, proximity to new highs, Relative Volume and Charts view aid triage, but need manual chart confirmation. No current market chart classification is invented by the policy update.
