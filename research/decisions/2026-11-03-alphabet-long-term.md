## Why the thesis merits diligence
- Google Search/YouTube have established advertising distribution and cash-producing ecosystems; AI-enhanced search may defend and expand utility but could **cannibalise ad formats and raise serving costs**.
- Enterprise Google Cloud + proprietary TPU systems supply an AI platform with revenue already scaling; distinguish repeatable service and compute revenue from **one-off or highly hardware-intensive TPU system sales**.
- AI can reinforce existing product reach: Search, Cloud, Workspace/consumer subscriptions, developer tooling, platform security and distribution.
- Strategic resilience is a hypothesis, **not proof of market-beating future returns**; track regulation, competition, data rights, energy availability and capex efficiency.

1. **Core holding hypothesis:** assess 5–10-year compounder economics, not only near-term technical momentum. Staging entry over a defined number of tranches can limit timing concentration but **does not prevent permanent capital loss**.
2. **Investment case:** build conservative/base/optimistic revenue, margins, depreciation, AI capex and free cash flow profiles for 2027–2030. Model returns on incremental infrastructure investment, not simply EPS multiples.
3. **Independent risk:** concentration in megacap platforms, dollar currency exposure, regulatory outcomes, Search AI disruption, TPU hardware margin/mix and financing/energy requirements.
4. **Execution responsibility:** adviser/investor selects final size, suitable purchase prices, custody, taxes and portfolio constraints. No automatic orders or performance claims in this repo.
5. **Monitoring triggers:** materially accelerating Search monetisation disruption, negative return on incremental cloud capex, deteriorating underlying cloud services growth, balance sheet strain or valuation substantially outrunning expected free cash flow.
