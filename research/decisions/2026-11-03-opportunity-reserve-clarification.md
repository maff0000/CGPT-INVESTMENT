# OPPORTUNITY-RESERVE-001 — Reserve capital and conviction thesis clarification
**Date:** 2026-11-03 Europe/London | **Status:** conceptual strategy clarification / no trades.

## Decision
The research programme should not assume fixed-income reserves are untouchable. The intended general structure is **liquid/gilt reserves to enable staged future equity participation**, including market-wide repricing or a prolonged crash. Capital preservation and sufficient liquidity remain critical, but the *eventual destination of suitable reserve capital* may be attractive businesses when valuation, fundamentals and market conditions meet pre-set gates.

**Explicit anti-pattern:** mechanically buy every 10% drop, call every lower price an opportunity, or exhaust reserve capital before the earnings/credit regime is understood. A bond maturity ladder is not a licence to ladder blindly down the equity market.

In parallel, Alphabet has an **owner high-conviction business thesis** covering its information discovery/indexing, platform distribution, advertising monetisation, custom TPUs, Gemini and Cloud. That thesis is recognised as exceptionally interesting **without asserting the company is provably the single strongest on the stock market or safe at all prices**. It is recorded separately as [STRATEGIC-003](2026-11-03-alphabet-full-stack-thesis.md).

## Method / authority
Two competing but coordinated uses for future authorised capital: **individual strategic-franchise accumulation** and **reserving liquidity for broad-market dislocations**. Both consume one overall portfolio risk budget. Stage deployment only after updated company value, market regime, cash needs and concentration review; no purchase quota or pre-authorised drawdown percentage.

See [public research policy](../../docs/opportunity-reserve-deployment.md) and [existing strategic staging policy](../../docs/strategic-staged-accumulation.md). This records **no personal asset figures, individual accounts, holdings or private manager instructions**.
