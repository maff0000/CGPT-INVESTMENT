# SCOUT-001 — Identify constructive bases before visible momentum
**Date:** 2026-11-05 Europe/London | **Decision:** adopt pre-breakout scouting as a pilot, preserving original filters and risk rules.

## Motivation
Strict Month Up and SMA50/SMA200 conditions can miss attractive businesses spending weeks in a quieter, tightening base. Seeking only confirmed momentum may introduce unnecessary entry lateness. A pre-breakout scout identifies and ranks such potential setups **before** the move.

## Adopted approach
- Retain QARP v2 **A, B and C unchanged**. Search **A plus separately disclosed fundamental/premium exceptions** to avoid the B/C Month Up bias.
- Retain **three primary chart groups**: BREAKING OUT, IN TREND, WATCH. Add WATCH substages **BASE FORMING → COILING → AT PIVOT**. These substages are *not* new purchase statuses or presumptive proof of accumulation.
- Prototype two independent historical-snapshot features: **10-day vs prior-40-day daily-range contraction** and **10-day vs prior-40-day share-volume dry-up**, plus actual support/resistance, pivot proximity, stock vs sector/broad-market RS and source-proven fundamental quality. The <=0.75/<=0.80 ratio hints are exploratory, NOT validated cutoffs.
- Use available Finviz Volatility, Pattern, High/Low and RVOL filters **as approximate discovery signals**. Elite advanced indicators/Find Similar Charts may help, subject to access. A one-day low RVOL does not prove a multiweek decline in supply.
- Promote to BREAKING OUT only when a meaningful daily closing break of recorded resistance and supporting participation are documented. Independently decide BUY/WAIT/REJECT after valuation/earnings/risk assessment.
- Record candidates before outcomes; measure future 5/20/60-session returns, failed breakouts, missed winners and sector biases. Never equate a hypothetical pattern with institutional ownership data.

## Explicit non-decision
No screener was run, no ticker was newly categorised, no trade placed, and no historical price/volume data was downloaded. The existing 22-company audit remains CHART PENDING until independently inspected. No private portfolio material is included.
