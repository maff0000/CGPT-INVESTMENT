# STRATEGIC-002 — Alphabet phased accumulation research framework
Date: **2026-11-03**, Europe/London. Status: **documented public research approach**; no investment or order approved.

**Decision:** Given Alphabet's long-horizon strategic candidate status, adopt a **conditional three-tranche study** for any future adviser-approved incremental amount: **35% initial** at a justified price, **30% on 10–15% valuation/price improvement if business thesis intact**, **35% after the next quarterly results and the accepted two-full-session cooling window** if the thesis and price remain acceptable.

**Critical:** The split and price-reference arithmetic are *illustrative research parameters*, **not** the owner's personal allocation, pending orders, private account balances, position details or manager instructions. No data concerning any actual holdings or losses is recorded.
