# ALL-SECTOR-001 — Weekly opportunity output from the other eight sectors
**Date:** 2026-11-03 Europe/London. **Status: adopted.**

Owner clarified that the previous three rotation lanes must not exclude the rest of the market. Target: **at least seven credible potential investments weekly** from the other sectors, including technology stocks pressured by AI fear, businesses deploying AI productively and businesses supporting agentic systems. The owner is willing to take measured risk and does not require seven purchases.

Approved architecture of research process (no software build/portfolio action):
- Parallel **all-other-sector** search across Technology, Communication Services, Financials, Consumer Discretionary, Energy, Materials, Real Estate, Utilities; old industrial/healthcare/staples lanes unchanged.
- Seven **fully named, sourced, current-price and event-tagged investment hypotheses**, not seven screener symbols. Expand search if short; **never fabricate to hit a quota**. Include diverse industries and at least one non-AI thesis.
- Distinguish **QARP strict passes** from **premium compounder/AI-adopter exceptions**. Price and earnings gates remain mandatory; an exception does not loosen the global v2 filters.
- Separate **Invest Now**, **watch/price**, **earnings event hold**; maintain thesis invalidation and subsequent returns. Even in negative technical sector rotation, a stock can qualify if its own business economics and expected valuation make sense.
- Exemplar firms **NET** (MCP/AI retrieval/security) and **ZS** (agentic Zero Trust/data protection) to be scrutinized on actual profits, valuations and revenue attribution, not on product claims alone.

**First recorded pilot: nine names from eight sectors; no purchases approved.** Next week refresh evidence and promote/reject with a dated record. See [working policy](../../docs/all-sector-opportunity-pipeline.md).
