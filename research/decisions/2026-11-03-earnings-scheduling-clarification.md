# EVENT-003 — Clarify unavailable future earnings dates without paralysing entry review
**Date:** 2026-11-03, Europe/London. **Status:** ADOPTED practical implementation of 7+2 earnings rule, v1.2. **No trades executed.**

The original 7-before and 2-complete-sessions-after gate was correctly adopted. A rigid interpretation of "next date unknown => permanently blocked" would nonetheless prohibit virtually every stock just after its Q3 results because many companies do not announce Q4 dates until weeks later.

**Decision:** If a company reported during the previous 30 calendar days, the after-results cooldown has completed, the company IR release/event calendar and independent near-term calendar were checked on the proposed decision day, and no next earnings event appears within **the following seven exchange sessions**, permit **provisional entry-review eligibility for 24 hours**. This never means the next earnings date has been verified. Recheck on the actual transaction day. Any conflict, calendar change or material event triggers reassessment. If less fresh results, apply more conservative UNKNOWN handling.

**Non-negotiable:** A named event blocks new purchases on the seven previous exchange sessions and release day, with two fully completed sessions after release; changing dates trigger re-evaluation. The provisional path is not automatic buy permission.

Evidence: [ALGN IR events](https://investor.aligntech.com/events) displayed Oct 28 Q3 in archives and no upcoming event, as of this review. [ISRG IR](https://investor.intuitivesurgical.com/investors/) similarly listed its latest Oct 20 Q3 but no announced future earnings date in the retrieved calendar. Absence of a listing is not affirmative proof no event exists.
