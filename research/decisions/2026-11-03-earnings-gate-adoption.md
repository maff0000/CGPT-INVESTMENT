# EARNINGS-001 — Adopt original seven-session earnings risk gate
Date: 2026-11-03 (Europe/London). Type: dated correction / adoption. Status: **ADOPTED PILOT RULE**.

## Reason
The founding repository recorded five exchange-trading sessions before earnings as a proposed fallback, and said the original numerical window was unavailable. A continuity review of the original research conversation showed an **explicit previously agreed rule: seven sessions before the release, with the first two complete post-results sessions blocked**. The five-session rule is a transcription/recovery error, not a change agreed by the research owner.

## Decision
- Restore **seven** exchange trading sessions preceding the release plus release day as a pre-entry blackout.
- Require **two fully completed** subsequent regular trading sessions and completed earnings/price review before entry *eligibility review*.
- For unusually volatile biotech or highly consequential binary events, the earlier proposed 10 pre/5 post extension remains a discretionary heightened-scrutiny rule; record its use.
- Missing/estimated/conflicting next-announcement information blocks entry review; desk research is allowed.
- No trade is authorised by satisfying an event gate. These pilot rules have no demonstrated predictive advantage.
