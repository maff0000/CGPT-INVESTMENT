## First-pass regime and causal scenarios — not stock verdicts
1. **Oil and geopolitics — high current materiality:** Crude down sharply as traders anticipate diplomacy; the risk of a failed ceasefire or renewed export disruptions remains. **Scenario A:** de-escalation persists → producer price realisations down, airline/travel/fuel users' operating costs potentially down. **Scenario B:** escalation returns → prices/transport premiums reprice upward. **Do not force a directional call.** Candidate mapping: **XOM (producer)**, **BKNG (travel exposure mainly indirectly through customers)** and **LIN (energy/feedstock and demand mix)**. For each, seek actual segment/hedging exposure before drawing cash-flow conclusions.
2. **Rates and nominal-vs-real discounting — high materiality:** 10Y 4.90%, 10Y real ~2.64%; single-day yields fell, but higher prospective capital costs still matter for **EQIX, NEE** (leverage/capex), **GOOGL/NET/ZS** (duration, capital intensity and valuation), and growth-driven industrial projects **GEV/EME**. Individual interest-rate hedges, contract backlog and cash-generating capacity can offset risks.
3. **Election policy regime — high uncertainty:** Nov3 election is fact; future AI infrastructure permitting, taxation, energy and federal spending **not yet known**. Nov4 sector ETF strength in consumer/financial versus energy and tech does **not establish** a sustainable rotation. **Countercase:** election relief fades; legislation stalls; commodity/geopolitical drivers dominate.
4. **Inflation/growth risk — moderate to high materiality:** CPI 3.7% y/y and PCE 3.6% y/y, weak September payroll gain; oil easing may help future inflation but the next jobs and CPI prints may produce sharp repricing. Map **consumer staples (MDLZ/PEP/UL)**, **consumer/discretionary (BKNG/OLLI)**, **payment businesses (V/CPAY)** and **industrials (EME/GEV)** to wage/cost/purchasing-power sensitivity with audited company figures.
5. **Credit / market breadth — unresolved:** high-yield OAS at 3.40 on Nov3 after widening in October; broad equities up Nov4 and VIX down. **Mixed data, not a fully corroborated risk-on regime.** Refresh credit and multi-session breadth before any risk-regime label is frozen.
