[Company Q3 earnings released Oct30](https://investor.exxonmobil.com/company-information/press-releases/detail/1213/exxonmobil-announces-third-quarter-2026-results): GAAP earnings $15.4bn, adjusted earnings $17.5bn, operating cash $25.7bn, reported free cash flow $19.1bn. An oil de-escalation story may already be reflected in stock prices, and Middle East disruption could recur. ## Independent challenge: why three good companies can be poor entries
- XOM: reported FCF and dividend history could be overinterpreted if the commodity cycle unwinds; conversely assume-no-war oil may be equally wrong.
- LIN: strong contracts and adjusted EPS can coexist with an overvalued share and weaker economic earnings than adjustments suggest.
- EQIX: demand tailwind and rising AFFO do not guarantee shareholder value if growth capex, refinancing and dilution absorb returns.
