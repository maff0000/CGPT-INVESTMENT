## What a completed review must include
A dated close and price source; SMA20/50/200 and slopes; base/resistance/highs; prior base length; breakout volume and relative volume; share-price relative performance versus S&P 500 and actual sector; sector/industry condition; earnings blackouts; downside/valuation; **chart group** (Breaking Out, In Trend or Watch); separate **BUY/WAIT/REJECT** research decision; and the next trigger.

Review possible breakouts from B/C and A recoveries first, trend-following entries next, Watch promotions afterwards. Do not assign a chart group from stale prices or from a company's strategic attractiveness.
