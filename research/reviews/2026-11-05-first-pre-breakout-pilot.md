## Scope, honest provenance and methodological limitations
- Used the [public VCP scanner](https://phuazz.github.io/vcp-screener/) with a seed universe of approximately 900 Russell 1000 shares. Its publicly visible **Nov 3 report used Nov 2 closing prices** for the six qualifying candidates. This is **not** a Finviz QARP A/B/C run, a whole-market scan, or our own independently computed VCP contraction and volume ratio.
- Rechecked the candidates against **Nov 4 completed closes** from [StockAnalysis daily OHLCV](https://stockanalysis.com/), together with selected company-origin results/earnings announcements. Snapshot price and pivot can be asynchronous. The external scanner's **pivot is a preliminary review level, not validated independently across the full 20–60-session price base**.
- [Griffin S&P 500 scanner](https://griffinresearchgroup.org/tools/bases/) independently reports a weak broad market in its Nov 4 scan, including SPY below its 50-day average; its site is a secondary scanner and its javascript result table was **not directly inspectable** for ticker-level verification.
- **Six external scout candidates are new RESEARCH LEADS only**, not additions to the canonical 22-company active watchlist; they have not passed all of our QARP v2 fundamental/sector checks. To enter the active universe, require documented quality, fair valuation, sector risk, price/volume confirmation and independent challenge.
- Today's classification is **triage/provisional**, not a completed OHLCV-derived CHART-001 classification. We have not measured our proposed last-10 vs prior-40 daily range/volume ratios, nor executed Finviz exports.

## What this experiment tells us
1. **Early coiling can be found** through an external scan, but it is **not equivalent to a verified QARP pass or a buy-now list**.
2. One **close near pivot (SJM)**, one **intraday false confirmation (ABNB)**, several **post-pivot rallies (CPAY/VOYA/DE)** and an **extended earnings stock (BDX)** already illustrate how critical **cutoff time** is.
3. **Earnings-gate controls rejected or postponed multiple superficially attractive setups**. This is not a bug: avoiding potentially adverse overnight event risk is part of the strategy.
4. **No independent complete 10/40 daily-range or volume contraction computation**, no full 22-name chart audit, and **no live Finviz A/B/C export** were completed. Do not advertise those actions as done or overinterpret the scanner's performance.
5. Candidate order for next manual validation: **SJM** (nearest entry; fundamental caveats), **CPAY** (post-Q3), **VOYA** (post-Q3 and extension), **DE** (cyclical), **ABNB/BDX** (event holds). Compare against **EME** (falling knife risk) and **ALGN** (late chase risk).
