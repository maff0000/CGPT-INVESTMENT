## Conflicts and accounting traps
- Alphabet's reported 2026 Q3 Google Cloud +103% includes hardware TPU system sales, and capex guidance $195–205bn plus Q3 negative FCF disrupt naive P/FCF and PE comparisons.
- NET forward PE 185x from expected non-GAAP earnings despite current GAAP loss. Don't confuse MCP implementation capability with incremental recurring revenue or penetration.
- ZS management describes agent protection strategically but new business ARR attributable specifically to agents is not disclosed in cited releases; share-based compensation may dilute FCF.
- EQIX is a REIT: **use AFFO/share, recurring vs growth capex and rates**, not GAAP PE.
- NEE announced Dominion transaction may change future share count/debt and rate base; don't mechanically apply past EPS.
- XOM's oil-price cycle and commodity-linked cash are not the same as recurring subscription revenue; a low PE can be peak-cycle pricing.
- Investors must distinguish *listed sector* from real-world exposure: LIN is Materials though it supplies semiconductors; EQIX is Real Estate though it supports AI; GOOGL is Communication Services despite AI platform.
- Price timestamps are not synchronized; dates/event times need immediate re-verification before buy. AI association **does not** itself count toward investability.
