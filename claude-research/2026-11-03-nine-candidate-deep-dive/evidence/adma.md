## 3. Product concentration, ASCENIV, plasma supply, yield enhancement

### Takeaway
ADMA is now essentially a one-product company. ASCENIV was about 83% of Q2 2026 revenue and BIVIGAM about 16%. The bull case rests on ASCENIV's niche position (high-titer RSV-antibody IG for hard-to-treat primary immunodeficiency) and on the 2025-approved yield-enhancement process, which lifted gross margin from about 55% to about 69–70%.

### Cited Findings
- Q2 2026 mix: ASCENIV $102.9M, BIVIGAM $19.4M, other about $2.1M, out of $124.4M total. — [Q2 2026 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001368514/000114036126031423/ef20079311_ex99-1.htm)
- Prior year: 2025 ASCENIV revenue was reported as $362.5M (+20%). — [plasma-collection / class-action summaries citing Culper vs ADMA](https://www.itiger.com/news/2622823713) (via search snippet, March 2026)
- Payer coverage: management cites "broad commercial payer coverage". A 127-patient real-world study (mostly patients switched from other IGs) showed fewer infection-related hospitalizations. The abstract was submitted to ACAAI in November 2026. — [Q2 2026 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001368514/000114036126031423/ef20079311_ex99-1.htm)
- Patents: ADMA says ASCENIV is "protected by numerous issued patents" in the US and internationally, with more applications pending. — same source.
- Plasma: ADMA divested three plasma collection centres in Q1 2026 for $5.0M in proceeds and an $8.0M pre-tax gain. Plasma centre operating expenses were $1.0M in Q2. — same source.
- Yield enhancement was approved in 2025. Gross margin was 69% in Q2 2026 vs 55% in Q2 2025 (one secondary source gives 69.4% vs 55.1%). — same source; [search snippet citing 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001368514/000114036126031423/ef20079311_ex99-1.htm)

### Inferences
- Selling plasma centres suggests ADMA leans more on third-party plasma, which is plentiful at the moment (management itself cites plasma oversupply). Cheap plasma helps COGS, but it also feeds the IG price competition that is hurting BIVIGAM.
- Part of the margin gain is mix: high-margin ASCENIV growing while lower-margin BIVIGAM shrinks. The split between yield enhancement and mix is not quantified by the company.

## 8. Thesis-breakers and what would invalidate a QARP case

### Takeaway
The QARP case depends on ASCENIV sell-through growth being real and durable, on margins holding at about 65%+, and on cash conversion staying strong. Any evidence against those (or regulatory or legal shocks) breaks it.

### Cited Findings (basis for the watch-list)
- The guidance implies a 2H ramp (see section 2). ADMA withdrew long-term guidance in May 2026. — [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4910063-adma-biologics-longstanding-bull-hits-the-reset-button-after-murky-q1-earnings)
- The channel-stuffing allegation and distributor inventory data. — [ADMA rebuttal](https://www.itiger.com/news/2622823713)

### Inferences
Invalidating evidence would be any of the following:
1. Q3 or Q4 2026 ASCENIV growth falls well below about 20% YoY, or a sequential decline. That would support Culper's pull-forward thesis.
2. Another cut to the FY2026 guidance on 2026-11-04, or 2027 commentary implying flat or declining EBITDA.
3. Gross margin falling back toward the 50s, from IG pricing or any ASCENIV discounting.
4. OCF falling materially below adjusted net income, or rising receivables or distributor inventory.
5. An FDA Form 483 with significant observations, a warning letter, or a supply interruption at Boca Raton.
6. Adverse findings in the class action, an SEC inquiry, a restatement or an auditor change.
7. Debt-funded buybacks continuing while EBITDA declines.
8. Material insider selling.

Supportive evidence would be: sequential ASCENIV growth with stable distributor days on hand, BIVIGAM stabilizing, reinstated multi-year guidance, and SG-001 progressing to IND.

### Gaps
- Q3 2026 data (2026-11-04) will resolve several of these. Revisit after the call.
