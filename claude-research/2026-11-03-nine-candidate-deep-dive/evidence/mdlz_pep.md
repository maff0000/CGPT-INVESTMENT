## Q2. Latest quarter detail (Q3 2026)

### Takeaway
MDLZ posted modest top-line growth that was volume-led (+1.8pp vol/mix, +0.3pp price). Adjusted gross margin rose sharply as cocoa deflation flowed through, and FY guidance was reaffirmed. PEP's growth was price-led (+3pp price, +0.5 vol), and North America organic growth was flat. Core operating margin fell 35bp, and core EPS guidance was cut by roughly half.

### Cited Findings
**MDLZ Q3 2026 (VERIFIED, [8-K Ex.99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001103982/000162828026068233/mdlzearningsreleasecontent.htm), 2026-10-29)**
- Net revenue was $9,967M, up 2.3% reported. Organic growth was +2.1%: vol/mix +1.8pp and pricing +0.3pp.
- Gross margin was 39.4% reported (+12.6pp; GAAP includes mark-to-market swings) and 34.2% adjusted (+3.8pp / +380bp).
- Operating margin was 17.6% reported (+10.0pp) and 13.9% adjusted (+1.9pp).
- Diluted EPS was $1.02 (+78.9%). Adjusted EPS was $0.78 (+6.8% reported, +5.5% in constant currency).
- YTD operating cash flow was $2,606M and YTD FCF was $1.7B. Shareholder returns were $2.8B over nine months: $1,927M of dividends and $845M of buybacks.
- Regions (reported / organic): LatAm $1,387M, +12.0% / +6.9%. AMEA $2,182M, +8.2% / +7.7%. Europe $3,552M, −3.3% / −2.1%. North America $2,846M, +1.1% / +1.6%. Emerging markets $4,072M, +4.9% / +4.1%. Developed markets $5,895M, +0.5% / +0.8%.
- FY2026 outlook was REAFFIRMED (unchanged): organic net revenue growth of ≥2%, adjusted EPS of flat to +5% in constant currency, FCF of about $3B, and FX adding about +1.5% to revenue and $0.043 to adjusted EPS.
- Vol/mix of +1.8% was the best since Q3 2023, and volume contributed more than price for the first time since late 2021 — [Investing.com, 2026-10-30](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/mondelez-earnings-analysis-questions-answered-and-next-catalysts-93CH-4988419) (secondary)

**PEP Q3 2026 (VERIFIED, [8-K Ex.99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000077476/000007747626000050/q320268-kxexhibit991.htm), 2026-10-08)**
- Net revenue was $25,274M (+5.6%). Organic growth was +3.1%, with M&A adding +1.7pp and FX +0.7pp. Organic volume was +0.5% and effective net pricing +3%. YTD organic growth was +2.7%.
- Segment organic growth (organic / volume / price): PFNA 0% / +0.5 / −1. PBNA 0% / −3 / +3. International Beverages Franchise +7%. EMEA +9% / +1 / +7. LatAm Foods +6%. APAC Foods +9% / +11 / −2.
- Gross margin was 54.4% on both a reported and core basis (versus 53.6% reported and 53.9% core a year earlier).
- Operating profit was $4,260M (+19%) at a 16.9% margin (+195bp). Core operating profit was $4,277M (+3%) at a 16.9% core margin (−35bp). Tariff refunds contributed about 4pts to core operating profit growth.
- GAAP EPS was $2.23 (+17%). Core EPS was $2.34 (+2%), and core constant-currency EPS rose +1.5%. YTD core EPS was $6.15 (+5%).
- FY2026 guidance was CUT. Core EPS growth is now +2.5% to +3.5%, versus the low end of +5% to +7% previously. Core constant-currency EPS growth is now +1% to +2%, versus the low end of +4% to +6%.
- Other FY2026 guidance: organic growth of about 3% (was 2–4%), net revenue of about +6% (raised), and a core tax rate of about 21% (was about 22%).
- Unchanged: capex below 5% of revenue, FCF conversion of at least 80%, and cash returns of $8.9B ($7.9B of dividends plus $1.0B of buybacks).
- YTD dividends paid were $5,935M and buybacks $739M.
- Restructuring and impairment charges were $192M in Q3 and $373M YTD. The 2019 productivity plan has been extended to the end of 2030. The CEO said additional structural cost actions "will be implemented in the coming months".
- Reasons for the cut: higher costs and investment in advertising, innovation and lower prices, plus fuel and packaging inflation — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/08/pepsico-earnings-guidance-cramer.html). Commodity hedges (typically 6–12 months) roll off in Q4 while commodity costs rise — [Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/08/pepsico-pep-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)
- Source conflict: Insider Monkey, citing Reuters, described the new guide as "1% to 2% EPS growth". That figure is the constant-currency core number, not headline core EPS — [Insider Monkey](https://www.insidermonkey.com/news/pepsico-just-cut-its-earnings-forecast-is-the-stock-still-worth-buying-1852078/)

### Inferences
- MDLZ's margin rebound is mostly input-cost driven, from cocoa deflation. Management is reinvesting the Q3 upside in Q4 advertising rather than raising guidance, which leaves room for upside in 2027.
- PEP's top-line acceleration is real, but it is being bought with price investment and advertising. The headline GAAP operating margin of +195bp flatters the picture, because the core margin actually fell.

## Q3. MDLZ specifics: cocoa, pricing and volume, emerging markets, M&A, buybacks, leverage

### Takeaway
MDLZ's thesis has flipped from a cocoa-cost squeeze in 2025 to cocoa tailwind and volume recovery in 2026. Cocoa is under $3,000/t versus the 2024 record of about $12,000/t. Management signals strong 2027 EPS growth, but says it is "premature" to call a return to its long-term top-line algorithm. Emerging markets and Asia are the growth engine. Leverage is moderate.

### Inferences
- **MDLZ invalidation evidence (watch at Q4 on 2027-01-28 and in FY27 guidance):**
  - (1) FY27 guidance for constant-currency adjusted EPS growth comes in below high single digits, despite cocoa deflation.
  - (2) Vol/mix turns negative again, especially in North America and Europe.
  - (3) ICE cocoa sustains a move well above about $5k/t on the 2026/27 deficit.
  - (4) A large leveraged acquisition (for example, a renewed Hershey bid) pushes debt/EBITDA above about 3.5x.
  - (5) EM growth (India, China) slows into low single digits.
- **PEP invalidation evidence (watch at the Q4 report around early Feb 2027 and the supply-chain update):**

  - (3) PBNA volume declines persist at −3% or worse with further share loss.
  - (4) FCF falls below about $8B, so that dividends exceed about 90% of FCF, or net leverage rises above about 3x.
  - (5) A dividend-growth freeze. PEP is a long-time dividend raiser, so a freeze would be a strong negative signal.
