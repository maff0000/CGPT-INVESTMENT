## 3. Multi-year quality (3–5 years)

## 7. What would invalidate the QARP thesis

### Takeaway
For ZWS, the thesis rests on continued core growth and margin expansion at a now-reasonable multiple. It breaks if core growth turns negative or margins give back gains as tariff and mix tailwinds fade. For FERG, the thesis rests on mid-single-digit organic growth, roughly 10% adjusted operating margins, and accretive M&A at about 20x forward earnings. It breaks if non-residential and data-centre demand rolls over before housing recovers, or if FloWorks or the bolt-ons push leverage and ROIC the wrong way.
