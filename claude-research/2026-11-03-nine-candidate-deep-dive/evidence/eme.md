The ROIC decline from about 47% to 37% TTM reflects acquisition goodwill (Miller plus 2026 deals) entering invested capital, not operating deterioration.
- FCF conversion above 100% in FY23–24 was helped by contract-liability (customer pre-billing) inflows during rapid backlog growth. That can reverse if growth slows, which is a key cyclical risk for an EPC contractor.

## 7. Risks and thesis-breakers (what would invalidate QARP)

### Takeaway
The QARP case (high ROIC, net cash, FCF conversion, about 18x forward) breaks if the data-centre cycle turns and reveals that margins and cash conversion were cyclical.

### Cited Findings
- Data centres make up about 47% of RPO (ESTIMATE from the $8.74B/$18.57B cited above) and about one-third of YTD revenue. — [MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/transcript-emcor-group-q3-earnings-call-highlights-2026-10-29/)
- Mechanical margin is compressing on contract mix (12.9%→12.1% YoY); Industrial Services margin is 1.8%, with turnarounds postponed; a $34M impairment hit an acquired business. — [Q3 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000105634/000010563426000132/eme-ex991_2026930xq3.htm); [MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/transcript-emcor-group-q3-earnings-call-highlights-2026-10-29/)
- Acquisition spend is rising (about $1.0B in FY25 and $0.8B in 9M 2026), and management says backlog amortization moderates near-term earnings contribution from deals. — [stockanalysis cash flow](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/eme/financials/cash-flow-statement/); [MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/transcript-emcor-group-q3-earnings-call-highlights-2026-10-29/)
- Weiss flags macro sensitivity to rates and non-residential construction spending. — [Weiss](https://weissratings.com/en/instant-news-alerts/emcor-group-inc-eme-down-7-7-should-i-exit-before-things-get-worse)

### Inferences: thesis-breakers to monitor (my framing)
1. **RPO stalls or declines sequentially** (especially data-centre RPO below $8.74B) for 2+ quarters, or the book-to-bill falls below 1.0.
2. **Hyperscaler capex guidance cuts** (MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL, META) or project deferrals cited on EME calls.
3. **Operating margin falls back below about 9%** (FY24–25 level) without a mix explanation, or US Electrical margin falls below about 11%.
4. **FCF conversion falls below about 75% of net income** for a full year, which would signal contract-liability unwind or working-capital strain.
5. **M&A drift:** acquisitions crowd out buybacks, rising goodwill impairments, or ROIC (stockanalysis definition) falling below about 25%.
6. **Balance sheet flips to sustained net debt** to fund deals.
7. **Valuation:** a re-rating above about 25x forward without an earnings upgrade removes the "reasonable price" leg.
8. **Fixed-price execution:** project write-downs on large mission-critical jobs (none reported in Q3 2026).

- No 10-K risk-factor review, customer-concentration percentage or short-interest data was gathered. No sell-side downgrade text was verified at source.
