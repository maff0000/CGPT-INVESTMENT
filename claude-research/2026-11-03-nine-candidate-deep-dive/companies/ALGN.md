## At a glance
- Core thesis: a net-cash franchise with ROIC of about 19%, buying back stock at 12.7x forward P/E and 10.5x EV/EBITDA, five-year lows on every multiple shown [VERIFIED, stockanalysis 2026-11-03].
- Main catch: the growth leg is missing. Revenue has been roughly flat at ~$3.7–4.0B since FY21, Q3 revenue grew only 1.9% against volume growth of 6.6%, revenue per case is falling, and imaging revenue fell 7.6% [VERIFIED, 8-K; history from stockanalysis].
- EPS growth is mostly cost and buybacks: non-GAAP operating margin rose 50 bp and shares are down 2.4% YoY [VERIFIED].
- The recovery shows up in volume, not in price. North American orthodontist shipments rose YoY for the first time in six quarters [VERIFIED]. The price-per-case decline and leased, lower-priced scanners point to defending share by giving up price [PROVISIONAL].
- QARP fit [PROVISIONAL]: cheap and high-return, but it fails the "improving growth" leg. Its quality is stable, not improving.

## Latest quarter
Q3 2026 (quarter ended 2026-09-30; released 2026-10-28), from the [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001097149/000109714926000077/algn-q326earningspressrele.htm) [VERIFIED] unless noted.

**Revenue**
- Total revenue $1,014.8M, +1.9% YoY and −3.9% QoQ.
- FX cost about $5.0M of revenue, so constant-currency growth was about +2.4% [PROVISIONAL].
- Clear Aligner revenue $839.3M (+4.2%); Imaging Systems & CAD/CAM revenue $175.5M (−7.6%).

**Volume and price**
- Aligner cases 690,635 (+6.6% YoY, −0.2% QoQ).
- Revenue per case $1,215, versus $1,245 in Q3'25 (about −2.4%, [PROVISIONAL]) and $1,260 in Q2'26.
- Doctors receiving shipments: 90,910 (88,155 a year earlier). Utilization 7.6 cases per doctor (7.3 a year earlier).

**GAAP results**
- Gross margin 68.7%; operating margin 16.7% (9.7% a year earlier); EPS $1.90 (versus $0.78).
- One-off: most of the GAAP operating margin jump reflects lower restructuring charges. Restructuring was $17.2M on the income statement and $28.5M in total across the non-GAAP reconciliation. Q4 restructuring is guided at $7–9M.

**Adjusted results**
- Gross margin 70.4%; operating margin 24.4% (23.9% a year earlier; 25.8% at constant currency).
- EPS $2.80, versus $2.61 (+7.3%, [PROVISIONAL]). FX cut EPS by about $0.24 YoY.

**Versus consensus**
- EPS $2.80 versus $2.77, and revenue $1.01B versus $1.00B; the fourth straight beat [REPORTED, 24/7 Wall St].
- Shares sold off after the release; no percentage was given [REPORTED].

**Cash and buybacks**
- Nine-month operating cash flow $526.7M. Q3-only operating cash flow, capex and FCF were not broken out [UNKNOWN].
- Cash $1,144.9M at 9/30/26.
- Q3 buybacks were about 627.3K shares at $164.73 (≈$103M, [PROVISIONAL]). $630.0M remains on the program; up to $300M is expected in Q4 and about $500M for FY26.

**Guidance**
- Q4: revenue $1,000–1,020M; aligner volume up low single digits; ASP down about 1%; GAAP operating margin about 20%, non-GAAP about 26%.
- FY26: revenue $4,110–4,130M, about +2–2.5% on FY25 of about $4.03B [PROVISIONAL]; non-GAAP operating margin about 23.7%; capex $125–150M.
- 2027: further revenue growth and about +100 bp operating margin, without assuming a consumer recovery [REPORTED, secondary source; not checked against the transcript].

**Demand commentary:** the CFO cited softer discretionary healthcare spending and weaker capital equipment investment [REPORTED, [Becker's](https://www.beckersdental.com/?p=28060)].

## Thesis-breakers
These are measurable triggers [PROVISIONAL]. Each is checked at Q4 2026 results (date [UNKNOWN]) unless noted.
- Aligner case volume turns negative YoY again, or North American orthodontist shipments fall again.
- Revenue per case falls more than about 3% YoY. Q3 was −2.4% and Q4 is guided at about −1%.
- FY27 non-GAAP operating margin is guided below FY26's roughly 23.7%, or the +100 bp step is withdrawn.
- Imaging/CAD-CAM revenue falls by double digits YoY. Scanners feed the aligner funnel.
- Q4 buybacks fall well short of the guided "up to $300M", or the authorization is cut.
- Evidence of share loss to DSO private-label or low-cost aligners.
- The CEO transition [REPORTED-by-journal] comes with a strategy reset, a guidance withdrawal or an unplanned departure of other executives.
