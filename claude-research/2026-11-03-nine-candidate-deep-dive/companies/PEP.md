## At a glance
- **Thesis in one line:** the highest-quality franchise of the three consumer names (ROIC 18–21% every year since 2021 [VERIFIED]) at a five-year-low multiple, while North America is repaired under activist pressure.
- **Main catch:** it is cheap for fundamental reasons. FY core EPS guidance was cut roughly in half, both North American segments grew 0% organically, and tariff refunds supplied ~4 pts of core operating profit growth [VERIFIED].
- **Funding the repair:** price cuts, advertising and SKU cuts come out of margin; the dividend uses ~76% of implied FCF [PROVISIONAL].
- **QARP fit [PROVISIONAL]:** quality and price legs present; the improving-momentum leg is not established beyond a one-month rebound from a 2020-era low.

## Latest quarter
Q3 2026 from the SEC 8-K Ex.99.1 [VERIFIED] unless noted.
- Net revenue $25,274M (+5.6%): **organic +3.1%** (volume +0.5%, effective net pricing +3%), M&A +1.7 pp, FX +0.7 pp. YTD organic +2.7%. Strongest organic since Q4 2023 and above the 2.75% FactSet consensus [REPORTED, CNBC].
- Versus consensus: core EPS $2.34 vs $2.29; revenue $25.27B vs $24.95B [REPORTED, Pulse 2.0].
- **Segments, organic / volume / price:** PFNA 0% / +0.5 / −1; **PBNA 0% / −3 / +3**; EMEA +9% / +1 / +7; APAC Foods +9% / +11 / −2; LatAm Foods +6%; Intl Beverages Franchise +7%.
- Gross margin 54.4% reported and core (prior year 53.6% reported, 53.9% core).
- **GAAP:** operating profit $4,260M (+19%), margin 16.9% (+195 bp); EPS $2.23 (+17%). The GAAP margin gain flatters the picture.
- **Core:** operating profit $4,277M (+3%), margin 16.9% (**−35 bp**); EPS $2.34 (+2%), constant currency +1.5%; YTD core EPS $6.15 (+5%).
- **Tariff refunds contributed ~4 pts to core operating profit growth.** Core operating profit therefore fell ~1% excluding refunds [PROVISIONAL calc]. Even "core" figures include the one-off.
- Restructuring and impairment $192M in Q3, $373M YTD; the 2019 productivity plan extended to end-2030, with more structural cost actions "in the coming months"; no dollar savings target [VERIFIED / REPORTED].
- **FY2026 guidance cut:** core EPS growth +2.5% to +3.5% (was low end of +5% to +7%); core cc EPS +1% to +2% (was low end of +4% to +6%). Organic ~3% (was 2–4%), net revenue ~+6% (raised), core tax ~21% (was ~22%). Unchanged: capex below 5% of revenue, FCF conversion ≥80%, cash returns $8.9B ($7.9B dividends + $1.0B buybacks).
- Reasons given: higher costs, investment in advertising, innovation and lower prices, fuel and packaging inflation [REPORTED, CNBC]; hedges (typically 6–12 months) roll off in Q4 while commodity costs rise [REPORTED, Fool transcript].
- Source conflict: one secondary outlet described the new guide as "1% to 2% EPS growth"; that is the constant-currency core figure, not headline core EPS [REPORTED, Insider Monkey citing Reuters].
- Management conceded it is "not competing well in soft drinks" in the US [REPORTED]. PFNA volume up low single digits after price resets [REPORTED].
