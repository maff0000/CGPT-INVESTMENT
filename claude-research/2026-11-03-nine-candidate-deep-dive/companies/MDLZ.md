## At a glance
- **Thesis in one line:** cocoa deflation (below $3,000/t against a ~$12,000/t 2024 record [REPORTED]) plus the first volume-led quarter since late 2021 sets up 2027 EPS recovery, with emerging markets as the growth engine.
- **Main catch:** the tailwind is cyclical and partly priced in. The stock rose ~12% in a month to just under its 52-week high, at 19.4x forward [VERIFIED].
- **Quality is middling:** ROIC 6.1–10.8% over 2021–25, leverage ~2.7–3.0x; the best feature is steady buybacks (share count −1.85% YoY) [VERIFIED].
- **QARP fit [PROVISIONAL]:** fair rather than cheap. EV/EBITDA is near the low end of its own history, but P/E and the rally leave little valuation cushion.

## Latest quarter

- Net revenue $9,967M, +2.3% reported; **organic +2.1% = vol/mix +1.8 pp + price +0.3 pp**. Vol/mix was the best since Q3 2023 [REPORTED, Investing.com].
- Versus consensus: adjusted EPS $0.78 vs $0.71; revenue $9.97B vs $9.98B (slight miss) [REPORTED, Investing.com].
- **GAAP:** gross margin 39.4% (+12.6 pp), operating margin 17.6% (+10.0 pp), diluted EPS $1.02 (+78.9%). These are inflated by commodity/FX **mark-to-market** swings and should not be read as recurring growth.
- **Adjusted:** gross margin 34.2% (+380 bp, cocoa deflation, volume leverage, productivity), operating margin 13.9% (+1.9 pp), EPS $0.78 (+6.8% reported, +5.5% constant currency).
- Regions, organic: AMEA +7.7%, LatAm +6.9%, North America +1.6% (volume flat [REPORTED]), **Europe −2.1%** (vol/mix +1.6 pts despite a 1 pt weather drag, but negative chocolate pricing expected for a couple of quarters [REPORTED]). EM +4.1%, DM +0.8%.
- Cash: YTD operating cash flow $2,606M, YTD FCF $1.7B; returns $2.8B (dividends $1,927M, buybacks $845M).
- FY2026 outlook **reaffirmed, not raised:** organic ≥2%, adjusted EPS flat to +5% cc, FCF ~$3B, FX +~1.5% to revenue.
- Call colour [REPORTED, Yahoo/GuruFocus]: India and China growing above 20%; management confident of "strong" 2027 EPS growth despite freight and commodity inflation; Q3 upside reinvested in Q4 advertising. US Halloween chocolate sales −$33.1M YoY (NIQ via Reuters) [REPORTED].

## Thesis-breakers
All [PROVISIONAL]; check dates are estimates where noted.
1. FY27 guidance (expected with Q4, reported 2027-01-28) gives constant-currency adjusted EPS growth below high single digits despite cocoa relief.
2. Vol/mix turns negative again in Q4 2026 or Q1 2027, especially in North America or Europe.
3. ICE cocoa holds well above ~$5,000/t on the projected ~190k t 2026/27 deficit (management expects the ~500k t 2025/26 surplus to absorb it [REPORTED]).
4. A large leveraged acquisition (for example a renewed Hershey bid) pushes debt/EBITDA above ~3.5x.
5. India/China/EM organic growth slows to low single digits.
- Confirming evidence would be volume-led organic growth of 3%+ with cocoa range-bound.
