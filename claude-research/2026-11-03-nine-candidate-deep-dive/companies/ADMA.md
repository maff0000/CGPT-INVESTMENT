## At a glance
- **Core thesis:** a high-margin niche immune globulin (ASCENIV) producer. Current figures are strong: ROIC 36%, Q2 gross margin 69% (55% a year earlier), TTM FCF $116M, and 13.5x forward P/E [VERIFIED, 8-K / stockanalysis].
- **Catch 1, concentration:** ASCENIV is about 83% of revenue and is made at one plant (Boca Raton) [VERIFIED / PROVISIONAL].
- **Catch 2, contested quality:**
  - The March 2026 Culper short report alleged channel stuffing, and a securities class action is pending [REPORTED].
  - FY26 revenue guidance was cut in May to $530–560M from more than $635M, and the 2027 and 2029 targets were withdrawn [REPORTED].
- **Catch 3, the H2 ramp:** guidance needs about $146–161M a quarter in H2, against Q2's $124.4M [PROVISIONAL].
- **QARP fit [PROVISIONAL]:** cheap on the numbers, but quality is contested. Predictability and governance are the missing legs.

## Latest quarter
Q2 2026 (quarter ended 2026-06-30; released 2026-08-05), from the [SEC EX-99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001368514/000114036126031423/ef20079311_ex99-1.htm) [VERIFIED] unless noted.

**Revenue**
- Total $124.4M, +2% YoY (1H $238.9M versus $236.8M).
- ASCENIV $102.9M, +23.5% YoY (about 83% of the total, [PROVISIONAL]).
- BIVIGAM $19.4M, −48.5% YoY, though up sequentially.
- Other about $2.1M.

**Margins and earnings**
- Gross margin 69% (55% a year earlier), from the yield-enhanced process approved in 2025 plus mix. The split between the two is not quantified.
- R&D $6.0M (versus $1.0M, for SG-001). SG&A $26.7M (versus $22.2M), including legal-related fees.
- Operating income $52.4M.
- GAAP: net income $37.8M (versus $34.2M); diluted EPS $0.16 (versus $0.14).
- Adjusted: net income $39.0M; adjusted EBITDA $61.8M (+22%).

**One-offs**
- 1H GAAP net income ($83.2M) exceeds adjusted ($79.6M) because of an $8.0M pre-tax gain on the Q1 sale of three plasma centres.
- The tax rate rose to 24.7% from 14.7%, so 2025 GAAP EPS comparisons are flattered. Net deferred tax assets were $70.0M.

**Cash and capital**
- 1H operating cash flow $87.8M (versus $1.5M); capex $4.8M; 1H FCF about $83M [PROVISIONAL].
- Cash $136.0M and debt about $196.6M, including a $125M JPM revolver draw.
- 1H buybacks $155.2M, about 13.8M shares YTD, including an ASR. Management targets at least $200M in 2026.
- Shares 225.4M; equity $408.2M (versus $477.3M at year-end 2025).

**Guidance (reiterated):** FY26 revenue $530–560M, adjusted EBITDA $265–300M, adjusted net income $170–200M.

**Reaction:** revenue was slightly below the $125.77M consensus and EPS was in line [REPORTED, Earnings Whispers]. Shares rose about 9% intraday to $10.10 on 2026-08-06 [REPORTED, RTTNews].

**Q1 2026 context (May)**

- Guidance cut, citing new IG entrants, plasma oversupply and discounting.

## Thesis-breakers
These are measurable triggers [PROVISIONAL]. The first checkpoint is Q3 on 2026-11-04, then Q4 2026.
- ASCENIV growth well below about 20% YoY, or a sequential decline. Either would support Culper's pull-forward thesis.
- Another FY26 guidance cut on 11-04, or 2027 commentary implying flat or declining EBITDA.
- Gross margin back toward the 50s, from IG price competition or ASCENIV discounting.
- OCF materially below adjusted net income, or rising receivables or distributor days-on-hand. The last data points were 84 days at 2026-01-05 and 48 days at 2026-03-22.
- An FDA Form 483 with significant observations, a warning letter, or a supply interruption at Boca Raton.
- Adverse developments in the class action, an SEC inquiry, a restatement or an auditor change.
- Debt-funded buybacks continuing while EBITDA declines.
- Material insider selling.
