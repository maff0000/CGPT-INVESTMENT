## At a glance
- **Core thesis:** quality is improving. NuVasive integration lifted adjusted EBITDA margin to 35.4% from 28.0%, and ROIC recovered from 7.5% (FY23) to 13.9%, with no borrowings [VERIFIED]. FCF has grown from about $104M (FY22) to $756M TTM [VERIFIED as published by stockanalysis].
- **Valuation:** below every FY21–FY25 year-end multiple: 15.6x forward P/E, 9.9x EV/EBITDA, 14.2x P/FCF [VERIFIED, stockanalysis 2026-11-03].
- **Main catch:**
  - Most growth since FY22 is acquired (NuVasive 2023, Nevro 2025). Organic growth looks mid-to-high single digit [PROVISIONAL].
  - Enabling Technologies (robots) fell about 26% and Nevro fell 14.3% in Q2 [REPORTED].
  - Q3 lands 2026-11-05, two days after this note.
- **QARP fit [PROVISIONAL]:** strong, pending 11-05. Returns and margins have been rising while the multiple fell.
