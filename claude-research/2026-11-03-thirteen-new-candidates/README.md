GEV's $16–17B FCF guide is mostly customer deposits (normalised ~$3.5–6B). XOM's 12.4x forward P/E assumes Brent at ~$104 persists, plus a refining windfall. NEE's 15.2x trailing P/E includes a $3.0B pre-tax hedge gain.
4. **Alphabet now funds capex from outside the business** [VERIFIED]: Q3 FCF was −$9.1B, there have been no 2026 buybacks, ~$85B of equity was raised in June plus $12.4B through an ATM, and long-term debt rose from $46.5B to $125.1B in nine months.

6. **BKNG is the cheapest name against its own history** (12.4x P/FCF, 14.0x forward) [VERIFIED]. Its catch is a Q4 guide of room nights +1–3% with EBITDA about flat, and a −23% 52-week return. **Claude's earlier suspicion that the ~$151 price was a data error was wrong**: Booking split 25-for-1 on 2026-04-02 [VERIFIED].
7. **The journal's price triggers mostly buy a smaller premium, not a QARP price** [PROVISIONAL]: ISRG ≤$375 is ~30x FY27E, and GEV ≤$725 is ~28x FY27E. UL's ~$65 condition was met at the close but stops being comparable when ~$11.5 a share of McCormick stock leaves at the deal's closing.
