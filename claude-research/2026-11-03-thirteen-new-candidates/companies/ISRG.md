## At a glance
- Thesis: a near-monopoly installed base of 12,094 da Vinci systems (+12%), with procedures +14% and non-GAAP EPS +25% in Q3. Net cash, ~24% ROIC and a falling share count [VERIFIED figures].
- Main catch: consensus EPS growth slows from ~26% (FY26) to ~11% (FY27). The company itself guides to a 3–5% cut in instruments and accessories (I&A) revenue per procedure from H1 2027, before mix effects [REPORTED 10-Q passage; vendor consensus]. At $440.75 that is 35x FY27E for ~11% growth.
- Second catch: credible US competitors are now cleared. Medtronic Hugo is cleared for urology (Dec 2025) and J&J Ottava has a De Novo grant (Jul 2026) [REPORTED]. The installed-base moat is not yet measurably dented.
- On its own history ISRG is the cheapest it has been in at least five years (trailing P/E 53 vs a 66–80x FY21–25 year-end range). Against peers it is still roughly twice the forward P/E of SYK, MDT and BSX [VERIFIED multiples].
- QARP fit [PROVISIONAL]: quality strong. Price fails the Discovery v2 thresholds (forward P/E under 25, P/FCF under 30). Momentum is mixed: +12.5% over 1 month vs SPY −2.0%, but −17.5% over 52 weeks.

## Thesis-breakers
[PROVISIONAL] measurable triggers, checked at each quarterly print.
- FY2026 da Vinci procedure growth below 14% (the guide's low end), or US da Vinci procedures below +8% in any quarter.
- I&A revenue per procedure down more than 5% YoY in 2027 after mix, i.e. the cut is worse than guided.
- Non-GAAP gross margin below 67% for two quarters (tariffs, price cuts, da Vinci 5 cost).
- da Vinci placements down YoY for two quarters, or the share of usage-based leases rising while utilisation falls.
- FDA clearance of Hugo for general surgery or hysterectomy, *and* Medtronic or J&J disclosing meaningful US placements.
- FY2027 consensus EPS below ~$11.75 (about 6% below $12.47).
- Court rejection of the settlement, or injunctive terms that restrict instrument-use limits or service practices.
- Diluted share count rising YoY (SBC no longer offset by buybacks).
