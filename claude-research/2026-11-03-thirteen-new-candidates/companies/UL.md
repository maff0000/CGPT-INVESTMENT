## At a glance
- Thesis in one line: an HPC-heavy staples franchise with improving volume (Q3 UVG +2.9%, FY UVG raised to ≥3%), 20% UOM, ~19% underlying ROIC and 100% cash conversion. It trades at ~17.8x FY2026 / ~16.6x FY2027 EUR consensus EPS converted at the ECB rate, below PG/CL at ~23–24x [VERIFIED inputs; PROVISIONAL ratios].
- Main catch: the Foods/McCormick Reverse Morris Trust will hand UL holders ~55% of a combined company by mid-2027. Today's $64.81 therefore includes roughly $11–12 per share of value that will leave the stock at closing [PROVISIONAL, at the March reference price]. Any fixed USD trigger (such as $65) is not comparable before and after closing.
- Second catch: the price reaction has been weak. The ADR fell 1.7% on the Q3 day (Oct 28) and 2.45% on Nov 3; 52-week −4.3%, although 1-month is +8.6% vs SPY −2.0% [VERIFIED prices; PROVISIONAL returns].
- Leverage: company net debt/underlying EBITDA 2.0x (FY2025) is at target, but vendor debt/equity (1.48 LT per Finviz, 1.75 total per stockanalysis) fails the QARP v2 <1 screen. The failure is structural (buyback-depleted equity) and not new [VERIFIED / PROVISIONAL].
- QARP fit [PROVISIONAL]: quality and price pass; momentum is mixed; balance-sheet screen fails; the event and deal path dominate the near-term setup.
