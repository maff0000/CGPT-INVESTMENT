## At a glance
- Thesis in one line: record bookings ($533M annualised, +35%) and a third guidance raise. FY26 AFFO/share is guided to $43.71–44.11, and the company's 2027–29 outlook is +9–12% a year [VERIFIED]. The stock trades at about 23x 2026 AFFO and about 21x 2027 if the outlook holds [PROVISIONAL].
- Main catch: growth is capital-hungry. 9M FCF was −$0.8B against $1.5B of dividends. Debt principal rose $3.8B to $25.2B in nine months, and capex is planned at $5–7B a year to 2029 [VERIFIED].
- Second catch: reported AFFO growth is flattered. Q3 AFFO/share was +18% as reported, but +12% normalised constant currency excluding a one-time atNorth JV fee of undisclosed size. Q2 also had one-time xScale fees [VERIFIED].
- Rates: implied average cost of debt is about 2.6%, while 2026 notes priced at 4.4–5.8%. Refinancing is a slow AFFO headwind, and the multiple is rate-sensitive [PROVISIONAL].
- QARP fit [PROVISIONAL]: quality franchise with low ROIC (5.7%). Price is fair versus own history (5-year average P/AFFO ~24.8x) and in line with DLR. Momentum is neutral (at the 50- and 200-day MAs; 52 weeks +19.8%).

## Thesis-breakers
[PROVISIONAL] triggers, checked at the Q4 print (est. mid-Feb 2027) and each quarter:
- 2027 AFFO/share guide below +8% normalised constant currency, or the long-term +9–12% outlook cut.
- Annualised gross bookings below $400M for two consecutive quarters.
- Net debt / annualised adjusted EBITDA above 5.0x on the company definition, or a credit rating outlook cut.
- Common equity issuance (ATM or forward) at below about 20x AFFO, which would dilute AFFO/share.
- Interconnection revenue growth below 7% YoY, or Fabric growth decelerating below 15%.
- Total capex above $7B in a year without a matching bookings increase. Recurring capex above 4% of revenue.
- Dividend above 60% of AFFO, or dividend growth cut below AFFO/share growth.
