## Thesis-breakers
[PROVISIONAL] measurable triggers, checked at each print and legal milestone:
- Net revenue growth (CC) below ~10% for two quarters, or FY27 guide cut below "low double digits".
- Client incentives growing more than 6 points faster than gross revenue for a full year, or incentives above ~30% of gross revenue.
- Cross-border ex intra-Europe below +8% (it was +14% in Q4 and +15% in October).
- Annual litigation provisions above ~$2.5B, a DOJ ruling or remedy restricting debit routing or incentive contracts, or rejection of the interchange settlement.
- CCCA passing either chamber, or attached to must-pass legislation.
- FCF below 85% of GAAP net income for a second year, or share-count reduction below 1.5%/year.
- Forward P/E above ~32x with no upward EPS revision (top of the bull assumption).
