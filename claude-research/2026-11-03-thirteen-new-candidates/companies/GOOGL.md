## At a glance
- Thesis in one line: Search is still growing (+12%), Cloud has doubled with a $561B backlog, and the core still earns a 31.8% operating margin while funding the largest capex programme in the group [VERIFIED figures; PROVISIONAL reading].
- Main catch: Alphabet now finances its capex from outside the business. Q3 FCF was −$9.1B. There have been no buybacks in 2026. It raised about $85B of equity in June and sold $12.4B through an ATM in Q3, and long-term debt rose from $46.5B to $125.1B in nine months [VERIFIED].
- Second catch: the cheap-looking multiples are distorted. Trailing EPS of $19.15 is inflated by SpaceX and Anthropic mark-to-market gains. On operating earnings the stock is about 26x TTM, not 14.6x, and EV/EBIT of 21x sits near its own FY21–25 median of 20x [PROVISIONAL].
- Momentum is poor: −12.6% on Oct 29, −18.5% over 1 month vs SPY −2.0%, about 17% below the 50- and 200-day MAs, RSI 22 [VERIFIED/PROVISIONAL].
- QARP fit [PROVISIONAL]: strong on quality, but only average on price once gains are stripped out. Momentum fails the screen. The 2027 depreciation step-up is the key unknown.

## Thesis-breakers
