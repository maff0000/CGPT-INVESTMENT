## Thesis-breakers
[PROVISIONAL] measurable triggers:
- Room-night growth below +3% for two consecutive quarters after Q4 2026 (i.e., Q1–Q2 2027), or full-year 2027 gross-bookings guidance below mid-single digits.
- Marketing above ~5.5% of gross bookings, or direct-channel mix falling below "mid-fifties". Either would signal paid or AI channels taking economics.
- Disclosed commission or take-rate concessions to AI platforms, or management saying AI-agent bookings carry lower margins.
- FTC resolution above the $1.0B accrual, or practice changes that reduce fee/price-display revenue. A second US/EU regulator action on fees or parity.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin (FY) below ~36% (FY25 36.9%).
- FCF falling below ~$8B in a year, or net debt above ~$8B while buybacks continue.
- Diluted share count shrinking by less than 4% a year (buyback capacity ceases to compound per-share value).
