  - Output ~5 GW a quarter, rising to 24 GW a year in 2028 [REPORTED, Utility Dive/Finsee].

- FY2026 guidance raised: revenue $46.5–47.5B, adjusted EBITDA margin 13.5–14.5%, FCF $16–17B (prior $11.5–12.5B), Power margin 19–21%, Wind loss $400–500M. Q4 FCF implied ~$0.7–1.7B [PROVISIONAL].

## Thesis-breakers
[PROVISIONAL] measurable triggers.
- Combined gas backlog plus reservations below 130 GW at year-end 2026, or any quarter with net slot exits.
- Reservation-to-order conversion stalling: firm gas GW flat for two quarters.
- Contract liabilities falling sequentially while Power EBITDA margin is below 19%, i.e. deposits unwinding without margin.
- Electrification organic orders negative for a second straight quarter, or book-to-bill below 1.0.
- Wind loss above the $500M guide, or new wind warranty or legal charges.

- FY27 consensus EPS falling below ~$23.
- Hyperscaler capex cuts: about 20% of gas demand comes from data centres, a shared exposure with EME.
