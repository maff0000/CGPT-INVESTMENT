## At a glance
- Thesis in one line: a scaled zero-trust platform ($3.77B ARR) with 80% non-GAAP gross margin and ~23% FCF margin, at 10.6x EV/sales and ~42–45x EV/FCF. That is below its own FY22–FY25 range and well below PANW, CRWD and DDOG [VERIFIED multiples; PROVISIONAL reading].
- Main catch: growth is decelerating. Headline +25% is ~20% organic (Red Canary added $141M ARR), and FY2027 guides ARR and revenue to only +16.6–17.5% [VERIFIED].
- Second catch: FCF after stock pay is about zero. FY26 FCF $779.1M vs SBC $841.6M gives −$62.5M on company definitions. Dilution runs ~3.5–3.8% a year with no buyback [PROVISIONAL arithmetic on VERIFIED data].
- AI/agent security has momentum (Security-for-AI bookings +50% QoQ, pipeline +75% [REPORTED]), but no dollar ARR is disclosed [VERIFIED by absence].
- QARP fit [PROVISIONAL]: the closest of the two high-multiple names to "reasonable price" on headline FCF. On SBC-adjusted cash it is not cheap. Momentum is strong over 1m (+14.5% vs SPY −2.0%) but the stock is −32% over 52 weeks and −13.6% from its Oct 26 high.
