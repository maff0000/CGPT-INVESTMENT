# Thirteen new candidates: cross-study summary — 2026-11-03

As of 2026-11-03, the thirteen names fall into three groups. The first group has proven quality and a price that already assumes it holds: **Visa (V), Linde (LIN), Intuitive Surgical (ISRG), Coca-Cola Europacific (CCEP) and Equinix (EQIX)**. Each trades at or above its own multiple history (EQIX is the exception, at about its five-year average), and in most cases the base-case arithmetic sits close to or below today's price. The second group looks cheap or fairly priced only because the headline numbers are flattered. **GOOGL**'s trailing P/E of 14.6x rests on SpaceX and Anthropic mark-to-market gains, and Alphabet now funds capex with equity and debt instead of buying back shares. **GEV**'s $16–17B FCF guide is mostly customer deposits. **XOM**'s 12.4x forward P/E is set on crisis-level Brent (~$104) and a refining windfall. **NEE**'s 15.2x trailing P/E includes a $3.0B pre-tax hedge gain. The third group sits on a near-term event that decides the case: **UL** (Investor Event 4 Nov, UK CMA phase 1 ~11 Nov), **NET** (Q3 on 5 Nov), **BKNG** (a $1.0B FTC charge whose terms are in a 10-Q not yet filed) and **ZS** (Q1 date not confirmed: 24 vs 27 Nov). On its own history the cheapest name is **BKNG** (12.4x P/FCF, 14.0x forward). Its catch is a Q4 guide of roughly flat EBITDA and poor momentum. Claude's earlier suspicion that BKNG's ~$151 price was a data error was **wrong**: Booking split 25-for-1 on 2026-04-02. Only two of the journal's price triggers imply a QARP-level multiple at the trigger price: ALGN (~13x forward, though its growth leg is missing) and EME (~17.5x FY27E). ISRG at $375 would still be ~30x FY27E, and GEV at $725 ~28x. UL's ~$65 trigger stops being comparable once the McCormick deal closes. Everything below is research framing, not personal investment advice or a buy/sell recommendation. Bear/base/bull figures are illustrative arithmetic, not price targets.

**Evidence labels used throughout (journal convention).** **[VERIFIED]** means sourced to a company release, SEC filing, or a dated market-data page. **[REPORTED]** means secondary press, call summaries, aggregators or analyst commentary not checked against a primary document. **[PROVISIONAL]** means interpretation or arithmetic by Claude, including all scenarios. **[UNKNOWN]** means not found, and it is left unfilled. Unless stated otherwise, prices and multiples are stockanalysis.com **closing** readings for 2026-11-03 (4:00 PM ET). ## Where the headline numbers mislead

- **GOOGL:** trailing EPS of $19.15 includes about $100B+ of pre-tax equity-security gains (SpaceX, Anthropic) over the TTM. On operating EPS of ~$10.65 the stock is ~26x, not 14.6x **[PROVISIONAL]**. "Buybacks" have become net issuance: $0 repurchased in 2026, ~$85B of equity in June, $12.4B through the ATM, and long-term debt up from $46.5B to $125.1B **[VERIFIED]**.
- **GEV:** 9M operating cash flow ($16.74B) roughly equals the working-capital inflow (~$16.97B), mostly down payments. Contract liabilities rose from $25.8B to $45.7B. Normalised FCF is ~$3.5–6B, not $16–17B. The GAAP P/E of 24 includes a $3.99B Prolec gain **[VERIFIED/PROVISIONAL]**.
- **XOM:** Q3 was earned with Brent at ~$104 (+62% YoY). Refining contributed ~52% of adjusted earnings ($9.05B of $17.5B). FCF includes a +$2.2B working-capital release, so it is ~$16.9B ex-WC. Mid-cycle arithmetic ($65–85 Brent) gives $78–149 a share **[VERIFIED inputs; PROVISIONAL]**.
- **NEE:** Q3 GAAP EPS of $2.28 includes a $3.0B pre-tax non-qualifying hedge gain (~$1.07 a share); adjusted EPS was $1.24. The trailing 15.2x P/E becomes ~21x on the adjusted guide **[VERIFIED/PROVISIONAL]**.
- **EQIX:** AFFO/share grew +18% as reported but +12% normalised, excluding an undisclosed one-time atNorth fee. Q2 also had one-time xScale fees. The clean FY26 multiple is ~23.8x, at the five-year average rather than below it **[VERIFIED/PROVISIONAL]**.
- **BKNG:** GAAP revenue fell 1% only because of a $900M FTC revenue reduction (part of a $1.0B charge). Adjusted revenue grew +9%. The "revenue miss" headline compares GAAP revenue to a consensus that excludes the charge **[VERIFIED/PROVISIONAL]**.
- **ISRG:** a $450M ($347M after-tax) antitrust charge cut GAAP EPS to $1.49 from $1.95. The release states its per-share impact three ways ($0.99, $1.00, $1.29). Ex-charge TTM EPS is ~$9.3 **[VERIFIED/PROVISIONAL]**.
- **LIN:** cash conversion is falling. FCF/NI went from 174% (FY21) to 72% (TTM), FCF has not grown since FY21 while capex rose 82%, and P/FCF of 46.5x is the highest in the FY21–25 series **[VERIFIED/PROVISIONAL]**.
- **NET / ZS:** stock pay absorbs the free cash flow. NET's TTM FCF of $342M against SBC of $508M gives −$166M, with SBC ~20% of revenue every year since FY22. ZS's FY26 FCF of $779.1M against SBC of $841.6M gives −$62.5M, with dilution of 3.5–3.8% a year and no buyback. ZS's vendor P/FCF (~43x) uses $852M FCF; on the company figure it is ~47x **[PROVISIONAL on VERIFIED data]**.
- **UL:** the trailing GAAP P/E of ~13x is inflated by the Ice Cream demerger gain (diluted EPS €4.32 vs underlying €3.08). Company FCF (€5.9bn) is ~15% below vendor OCF−capex, so P/FCF is ~20.7x, not 17.4x **[VERIFIED/PROVISIONAL]**.
- Smaller items: V excludes $1.72B of FY26 litigation provisions, and litigation charges recur most years. XOM's GAAP figures also carry about −$2.5B of hedge-versus-inventory timing effects **[VERIFIED]**.
