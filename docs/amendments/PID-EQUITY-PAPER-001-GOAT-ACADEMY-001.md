## 1. Sponsor intent and product outcome

Build GOAT Academy as a **learning research organisation**, not simply a sequence of trade selectors. Six native specialist Bots—E-Bot Explorer, S-Bot Strategist, R-Bot Risk, M-Bot Manager, T-Bot Trader and NEO independent reviewer—complete an evidence-linked chain; every skill is assessed, retains applicable experience and can propose bounded, versioned improvement. Reuse existing offline `paper-bots` code, GitHub doctrine and decisions. GrokBot controls engineering delivery; the six product Bots are **not** the Engineer/Auditor/Tester/DevSecOps delivery roles.

Success combines: (a) truthful full-chain handoffs; (b) high-volume, prospective **forecast and shadow** experience; (c) capacity to sustain **at least ten qualified IBKR PAPER trade openings per US market session**, without a quota or forced signals; (d) individual and team **Reliability / Judgement / Edge** assessments; (e) durable explicit skill playbooks, independent exam and NEO; (f) shadow champion/challenger evaluation, probation and rollback; and (g) a simple human-auditable four-screen GUI. Paper activity is **not** a proven edge or permission for live capital.

The 10+ target is engineering **capacity**, not a requirement to fabricate trades. Report shortfall causes honestly: data, strategy, real risk vetoes, account capacity, API restrictions, scheduler, source rights or broker reconciliation. Prospectively observe rejected/abstained names. Respect days-to-weeks strategies and capital-days; do not shorten positions to obtain fast labels. The previously agreed 20-session/50-synthetic-lifecycle operational target remains useful evidence, not a forced trading quota.

## 2. Simplest architecture consistent with the accepted design

Three logical records, **not three competing sources of truth**:
1. **Journal:** extend the private canonical append-only event ledger for signed-as-received, source-linked forecasts, decisions, no-trades, orders, fills, outcomes, score events and lessons. Independent assessor/scorer reads immutable decision-time evidence. Hash chains alone do not prove authorship (private issue #25).
2. **Exam:** independent server-side regression and held-out cases with graders, controlled traps, rotating challenges and drift reports. Exam answers, scorer, risk policy and promotion gate are not accessible to the shared Grok Bot computer.
3. **Playbook:** GitHub versioned per-Bot *approved procedural lessons* (rule, applicability, evidence, incompatible versions, status, rollback). Each decision records the exact playbook digest and skill version. Grok native memory is working context, not regulatory/trading rule authority. No implicit model fine-tuning.

Preserve existing typed ledger, original event IDs/semantics, schemas and replay; additive versioned extension only after contract review. Do **not** rewrite v0.1-v0.3 histories, QARP manifest or accepted source. Ordinary database indexes/views are not second truth.

Score every Bot on **Reliability** (exams, correct role/rules, deterministic safety), **Judgement** (properly scored, timestamped and calibrated probability forecasts vs an agreed baseline) and **Edge** (incremental decision value on matched cohorts with realistic costs). Report numerator, denominator, market sessions, horizon, uncertainty, version and source. Never collapse into one flattering score. Rejections and abstentions remain in the observation set; unobserved future outcomes are `PENDING`, not wins/losses. No false attribution of M-Bot's position size or T-Bot execution to S/R predictions.

## 3. Skill lifecycle and appropriate degree of autonomy

`IDEA → EXAM / SHADOW TEST → INCONCLUSIVE | REJECTED | SHADOW_ACCEPTED → PAPER_CANDIDATE → PAPER_APPROVED → PROBATION → LEARNT | ROLLED_BACK`. Preserve every challenger result and original candidate decision, including inconclusive and failed tests; NEO cannot mark its own evaluation as authority. Use rotating holdouts and independent paired forward trials with date/sector/regime clustering and predeclared scoring. Proposed 20 sessions / 60 disagreements / positive lower confidence bound / both halves / drop-best-day are **starting design hypotheses**, NOT validated universal statistical rules. Validate calibration, error rates, power, repeated testing and false promotion using offline null cases before any autonomous research promotion.

**Standing autonomy after bounded WO acceptance:** Bots may automatically propose, run exams and shadow challengers and reject/continue experiments. The evaluation service may automatically mark a challenger `SHADOW_ACCEPTED` only for a specifically preauthorised non-executable research-method class and a separately accepted, frozen scorer/promotion rule. It may not unilaterally elevate a method to influence paper order eligibility or change any risk/stop/broker/account constraint. Such promotions need independent review and appropriate Matt/CGPT authority. Technical bug fixes and scorer changes follow normal engineering review; a green exam is not a licence to silently merge code.

No hidden automatic modification of production skill instructions, base model, frozen strategy, runtime permissions or risk limits. Maintain version-pinned champion and shadow challenger, equal prospectively captured inputs, randomised invocation order where feasible, and record unknown model assignment. Grok's native model choice is not pinned or independently proved equal between Bots. All execution claims are separately evidenced.
