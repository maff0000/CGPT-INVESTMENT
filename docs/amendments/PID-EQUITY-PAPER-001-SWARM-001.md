## 1. Architectural change, not an implementation

The PID's six specialist roles remain, but evolve from primarily deterministic paper-workflow functions to six **native, individually identifiable AI agents** with autonomy to investigate, reason, collaborate, challenge and propose improved methods. Existing code, fixed strategies and schemas are reference implementations and minimum safety baselines, not the entirety of the roles.

The organisation is **The Rogue Alliance**. Matt sets the investment objectives and authorises policy. CGPT owns architecture, amendments and acceptance. M-Bot is the operational team manager; NEO independently observes and feeds evidence-backed performance critiques; R-Bot independently challenges risk; E-Bot explores; S-Bot develops strategy; T-Bot investigates and performs only separately authorised execution. No AI role may modify its own permissions.

This amendment supplements the PID's **What — six bounded specialist roles**, **Delivery ownership** and learning doctrine. Where the old text appears to restrict S-Bot to only owner-approved deterministic predicates, interpret that restriction as applying to **promotion/authorised execution**, not research invention. Where M-Bot is described as a paper-decision coordinator, that remains the existing implemented baseline pending a native swarm-management increment. NEO retains read-only independence and cannot enact its recommendations.

## 2. Preserved policy and hard stops

All current controls remain intact until separately changed through a documented owner/Architect decision:

- PAPER ONLY; no live trading or production broker write credentials.
- G0 remains OPEN and provider rights remain UNVERIFIED; source discovery does not establish licensing.
- QARP-ROTATION-001 and closed event catalogs remain frozen at existing approved versions.
- R-Bot has no independent power to widen risk; the non-LLM deterministic risk gate keeps denial authority.
- No unapproved stop, entry, exit, trailing, sizing, capital-occupation or account exposure parameter.
- Native identity and verified transport have not been demonstrated; Python role labels do not authenticate native teammates.
- No self-merging, self-accepting, irreversible ledger mutation or silent strategy/prompt promotion.
- Public GitHub remains sanitised. Secrets, licensed feeds and personal/account records remain private.
- Every operational change follows the existing git-tracked WO, independent audit, exact-head Architect acceptance, ordinary merge and closure chain. No work-order dispatch by this amendment alone.

## 3. New minimum system requirements (to be built incrementally)

1. Native team identities with role-specific read/write authority enforced outside prompts.
2. M-Bot task assignment, dependency tracking, conflict resolution and bounded research scheduling, with source/evidence links in the ledger.
3. E/S/R/T independent analysis and mandatory truthful hand-offs; R-Bot and NEO can publish dissent without M-Bot censorship.
4. NEO read-only review across M-Bot and all specialists; recommendations require visible M-Bot response and independent review before promotion.
5. Persistent, versioned, provenance-scoped learning memory derived from evidence; no self-editing canonical prompts/policies or unreviewed memory poisoning.
6. Open-ended read-only source discovery with controlled source onboarding; no automatic production trust or rights escalation.
7. A distinction between an append-only decision-evidence ledger and independently reconciled financial accounting.
8. Objective evaluation of swarm coordination, calibration, missed opportunities, capital efficiency and downside risk; no compulsory trade quota or simple raw-profit reward hacking.
