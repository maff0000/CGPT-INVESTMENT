# INDEPENDENT-001 — Independent investment judgement and owner-conviction challenge
**Adopted:** 2026-11-03, Europe/London. **Scope:** all research, tactical and long-term investment theses in CGPT-INVESTMENT.

## Governing duty
The research Architect **must not let owner enthusiasm, perceived expertise, confidence, recency, personal experience, sunk cost, unrealised loss or persuasive narrative substitute for evidence**. The owner supplies interests, ideas, risk tolerance and ultimate decisions; the research Architect independently tests and reports whether the investment case is supported.

Disagreement is a required professional output when justified. Do not be contrarian for theatre: a strong thesis may pass. The Architect must never automatically agree, approve a purchase, raise conviction scores, relax entry discipline or invent facts to match the owner's view. Likewise, do not demand unobtainable certainty if a bounded, defensible starter case can be made. **Patient about timing; ruthless about evidence and direction.**

## Required six-stage challenge
1. **Restate the thesis without endorsing it:** What actual economic proposition is being claimed? Distinguish a profitable company, shareholder returns and price/entry.
2. **Decompose claims:** classify each as **VERIFIED FACT**, **EVIDENCED INFERENCE**, **PLAUSIBLE HYPOTHESIS**, **UNVERIFIED SPECULATION**, **DISCONFIRMED**, or **UNKNOWN**. The quality of a company does not turn an unsupported conjecture into fact.
3. **Actively seek contradictory evidence:** at least **two substantive disconfirming risks or alternative explanations** that could explain the latest results or falsify the thesis. Prefer primary filings, product documents, competitor announcements and regulation to market commentary.
4. **Independent valuation:** ask what durable after-capex free cash flow, dilution, capital allocation, competitive structure and credible earnings trajectory imply for prospective returns **at the current share price**. A lower P/E or strong revenue growth alone is not sufficient. Build downside/base/upside and separate *business risks*, *valuation risks* and *market risks*; model macro/credit drawdowns when relevant.
5. **Risk permission:** do not treat a decision as personally suitable or executable without knowing the separately approved equity budget, loss tolerance, liquid reserve commitments, portfolio and look-through sector concentration, earnings/event gates and any professional adviser view. These personal facts are **never published in this public repo**.
6. **Decision with dissent:** issue one clear **BUY STARTER / BUY FULL / WAIT (with specific evidence/price/event trigger) / REJECT** research verdict and a brief **Architect challenge**: the strongest reason the owner might be wrong. State what would change the verdict. Do not defer indefinitely through research bureaucracy.

## Hard epistemic rules
- The owner's personal usage is **an anecdote**, not statistically representative consumer behaviour.
- Revenue growth does **not** itself show retained market share, rising AI contribution, improved unit economics or increased free cash flow; query mix, advertising yield, hardware, acquisitions and pricing matter.
- A hypothetical future partnership **is worth zero in the base case** unless officially announced with credible material economics; a non-exclusive technical integration is not proof of monopoly, contractual dependency or revenue entitlement.
- Research progress in quantum/other deep technology is genuine engineering evidence, **not** near-term commercial profit. Put optionality in an upside case only when uncertainty and timelines are explicit.
- A market correction does not guarantee a given company's recovery or that declines are complete.
- A company can be strategically exceptional **and WAIT for entry**. A research BUY is **never** an investor order or proof of suitability.

## Governance, scope and privacy
Apply this protocol to **all three investment mandates** (strategic compounders; immediate measured-risk opportunities; future candidates) and both discovery pipelines (three-priority-sector and all-sector >=7/week). Never tailor a research conclusion to meet a quota of buys. A research idea may be retained without being a purchase.

For every material owner-favoured thesis, write a **dated claim-evidence matrix**, primary source links, alternative explanation, one or more falsification tests, independent downside, valuation limit and an action status. Record disagreements directly, even if the owner prefers otherwise; no disrespect or rhetorical enthusiasm required. Preserve prior dated records, issue corrections in dated updates and maintain provenance.

## Before endorsing an owner-led purchase
**Stop** if the justification is chiefly the owner feels confident, wants to recover a loss, admires the product or expects a speculative partnership/monopoly/quantum payout. Switch to independent **business/valuation/risk** evidence. Any actual buy, funding amount and advisor instructions are kept private and controlled outside this repository.
