# QARP research methodology
Baseline recovered on 2026-11-03. QARP means quality at a reasonable price, with improving sector and stock momentum.

## Sequence and decision separation
1. Identify one emerging sector and two established rotation sectors.
2. Inspect industries: sector-level weakness may conceal improving breadth.
3. Discover financially credible businesses through the v2 screen and explicitly recorded near-misses.
4. Verify quality over 3–5 years: ROIC, cash conversion, organic growth, margins, diluted shares, management execution and leverage.
5. Estimate bear/base/bull fair value using sector-appropriate methods, peer comparisons and history. Explain assumptions and valuation date.
6. Assess absolute and relative stock momentum without substituting momentum for valuation.
7. Apply earnings and material-event gates.
8. Decide advance research, wait, reject or revisit after a named event.

Discovery, investment approval and capital deployment are distinct. This repository implements discovery and research only. No screening threshold establishes intrinsic value or predicts a price increase.

## Business exposure and near-misses
Record revenue/end-market exposure and common drivers across names. An industrial label does not diversify multiple companies exposed to AI/data-centre capital spending. Check acquisitions, financing, guidance, organic versus acquired growth, commodity pass-through and non-recurring benefits.

Preserve near-misses with the failed filter, source, magnitude, rationale and reviewer decision. Do not silently label them strict passes. Minimum dollar liquidity is an additional review field: average shares traded × a clearly dated representative price is only an approximation; a series of daily price × volume observations is preferable. A numerical minimum remains undecided.

## Valuation and resilience
Assess free cash flow after recurring investment needs, stock compensation and dilution, working-capital swings, debt maturity and refinancing exposure. Separate GAAP from adjusted metrics and actuals from forecasts. Record sensitivity to growth, margins and discount/multiple assumptions. Desired entry and margin of safety remain UNKNOWN until a documented valuation exists.

Banks/insurers require capital and credit analysis; REITs require FFO/AFFO and property/refinancing analysis; commodity producers require cycle-normalised economics; utilities require financing/capital-expenditure analysis. Do not force these into a generic operating-company screen.

## Proposed scoring, retained from the discussion
| Dimension | Maximum |
|---|---:|
| Business quality and financial strength | 30 |
| Valuation and margin of safety | 25 |
| Sector relative strength | 20 |
| Stock momentum | 15 |
| Catalysts and earnings outlook | 10 |

These weights are PROPOSED and unvalidated. They prioritise research, not trades. Do not score unknown evidence as zero or calculate a comparable total with missing dimensions; mark incomplete and show coverage. A high score never overrides an earnings block or missing valuation. Record scoring rationale and version before testing results.
