# Investment strategy — three complementary mandates
**Version:** STRATEGY-001 | **Adopted:** 2026-11-03 (Europe/London) | **Scope:** public research and decision discipline only.

**Mandatory decision authority:** any high-conviction or owner-nominated investment must pass [INDEPENDENT-001](independent-investment-challenge.md): independent fact checking, at least two counterarguments, after-capex valuation and downside, earnings/risk permissions, and a reasoned dissenting WAIT/REJECT when warranted. The owner supplies ideas; the research Architect owns the judgement. Business conviction never itself authorises a purchase.

## Objective
Use disciplined research to identify **durable, multi-year compounding ownership opportunities**, **measured-risk purchases available now**, and **future candidates with clear catalysts or attractive entry conditions**. The goal is rational deployment of capital over time, **not a perpetual research machine, forced weekly trading or speculative bets**.

The three mandates have distinct decision clocks, but are not mutually exclusive: **investment horizon** (strategic versus opportunity-driven) and **entry readiness** (NOW, LATER, BLOCKED, REJECTED) are separate fields. A strategic business can be a NOW staged-buy candidate or a WAIT-for-price candidate without ceasing to be strategic.

| Mandate / primary classification | Purpose | Typical horizon / assessment | Required result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Strategic compounders** | Durable franchises with defensible reinvestment opportunities, pricing power and compounding economics; suited to long-horizon wealth-management consideration | **5–10 years** as a working horizon; review fundamental thesis, durable free cash flow, capital intensity and industry structure | Explicit long-term OWN/ACCUMULATE CONSIDERATION or WAIT/REJECT, staged-entry scenarios and annual or material-event thesis checks |
| **2. Immediate opportunities** | Available **today or soon** at a price where forecast reward justifies a quantified, tolerable risk; can be cyclical, growth, recovery or quality | Price-aware purchase decision with the **7-before/2-after** earnings gate; reassess frequently when price or catalyst changes | **CONDITIONAL BUY STARTER / BUY FULL / WAIT / REJECT**, price ceiling, bear/base/bull and size/risk rationale |
| **3. Future candidates** | Valuable businesses whose current valuation, event risk, momentum or operating evidence is not yet attractive | Date/event/price-driven watchlist; review at defined triggers | Precise trigger for promotion, change in thesis, evidence gap, review date and invalidation conditions |

## Liquidity and opportunity reserve architecture
A proportion of high-quality gilts/maturing liquid assets may be held as **deployable opportunity capital**, not necessarily a permanent long-run bond weight. Evaluate two complementary, risk-budgeted equity deployment regimes: deliberate accumulation of strategic compounders at attractive valuations, and additional purchases during meaningful broad-market dislocations. **Do not automatically buy every percentage decline in a bear market**: require updated fundamental, valuation, market regime and liquidity/portfolio checks. A bond **maturity ladder** and an equity **falling-price ladder** are different things. See [opportunity reserve policy](opportunity-reserve-deployment.md).

## Strategic compounder lens (distinct from short-term rotation)
- Test long-run earnings and owner-cash compounding, not just a low forward P/E, short-term price momentum or current sector leadership.
- Evaluate market structure, competitive advantages, customers' switching costs, pricing power, management allocation, dilution, return on incremental invested capital and balance-sheet resilience.
- Analyse base/bear/bull 5–10-year **earnings and cash-generation paths**; short-term multiple compression, business-model disruption and permanent capital impairment remain real risks.
- Consider **staged accumulation** to moderate timing risk: initial conviction tranche, subsequent tranches on favourable price or verified operational progress, and an explicit *do not add* condition. Averaging down is not automatic; thesis must still hold.
- A durable business is **not worth any price**. Use valuation, realistic shareholder yield, capital required for growth, and upside/downside to decide whether to start, hold off or increase the studied allocation.
- Strategic conviction does not imply permanent holding irrespective of fundamentals. Change the thesis if economics deteriorate; avoid using the multi-year label to rationalise a failed position.

Its cloud/search economics deserve serious review; exceptionally high AI capex and possible Search monetisation disruption require independent valuation. A short-term Technology selloff is **not** alone a reason to reject a long-term compounder. Conversely, a compelling AI story is **not** a price-independent buy.
- **ALGN and EME — IMMEDIATE conditional starter research**, tied to *historic* reference price ceilings and earnings/event/position-risk checks. Their labels and quotes expire as market facts change; see [dated watchlist](../research/watchlists/2026-11-03-now-later-cycle-03.md).
- **ISRG, GEV, NET, ZS and others — FUTURE or active research candidates depending on valuation and events**. A company may be promoted to strategic consideration only after long-horizon business and free-cash-flow evidence supports it. Do not confuse a future candidate with a rejected business.

## Decision and implementation boundaries
1. **Research discovery:** original three-sector rotation plus [all-other-sector weekly >=7 candidate process](all-sector-opportunity-pipeline.md). The quota measures *genuine ideas reviewed*, not required purchases or strategic holdings.
2. **Classification:** each current review records primary mandate, action state, time horizon, quote timestamp, relevant evidence, event gate and what would change the decision. Strategic and immediate categorisation can overlap; no duplicate symbol counted as a fresh idea without new evidence.
3. **Capital risk:** quantified scenarios and overlap with existing exposures, FX, liquidity and investor-defined risk budget. Do not publicly record any personal capital balances, holdings, target weights, adviser identity or transactions.
4. **Earnings:** accepted **seven exchange trading sessions before the release plus release day** blocked for new entry/addition review, and **two fully completed sessions afterwards**, with v1.2 calendar clarification. This regulates initiation, **not research or long-term thesis monitoring**. Do not turn an earnings block into an indefinite ban.
5. **Execution authority:** decisions are **hypothetical public investment research**. A personal investor and relevant adviser separately choose actual securities, quantities, timing, taxation, custody and suitability. **No automated orders or inference that a purchase occurred.**
6. **Outcomes:** judge both 1/4/12-week *opportunity* calls and multi-year *strategic* investment theses against their dated assumptions, relevant benchmarks and full company economics. Do not infer alpha or guaranteed compounding from one selected example.

## Operating standard
Each weekly programme should identify **at least seven substantiated potential investments from non-priority sectors** and separately review the existing rotation lists. Present **(a) potential long-horizon compounders, (b) actionable measured-risk opportunities, and (c) priced/event-triggered future candidates** without forcing any bucket to contain a specified number of buys. The most useful output is **a reasoned ownership decision and what would change it**.
