# Sector and industry rotation
## Fixed structure, revisable selections
Research one emerging sector and two established rotation sectors. Initial lanes: Industrials, Healthcare and Consumer Defensive. Their current status must be re-established each week. Target 3–5 candidates per lane, allowing fewer; the first nine remain the dated starting set.

## Evidence at a common cutoff
Collect 1-week, 1-month and 3-month returns for sectors, the S&P 500 benchmark and relevant industries. State whether returns are price or total returns, source methodology and start/end dates. Do not compare incompatible periods or assume a sector-group aggregate equals an investable ETF.

Define simple relative return as sector return minus benchmark return, in percentage points, over the same dates. Distinguish it from a compounded relative-price ratio. Check industry participation and, where available, constituent breadth above SMA50/SMA200. Record breadth denominator and missing constituents.

## Working classifications
| State | Evidence sought | Research response |
|---|---|---|
| Declining | Persistent negative returns and relative weakness | Low priority; explicit recovery thesis required |
| Emerging / early | Improving short-term returns/breadth; longer-term confirmation absent | Industry-first investigation |
| Established / leading | Positive 1- and 3-month performance, benchmark outperformance and broad participation | Search for overlooked or newly participating quality companies |
| Weakening | Deteriorating recent relative strength or breadth | Reassess priority and thesis |

These are qualitative working definitions, not a validated timing model. One positive week is insufficient to confirm rotation. Price strength is not direct proof of institutional inflows; flows, if discussed, need their own source.

## Initial industrial focus
Prioritise investigation of Industrial Distribution, Pollution & Treatment Controls, and Security & Protection Services. Keep Engineering & Construction as a recovery watch. Assess Waste Management valuation after appreciation and Electrical Equipment downside/cycle exposure. These priorities come from the historical discussion, not a fresh industry ranking.

Strengthening evidence: successive weekly outperformance, better 1-/3-month relative returns, increasing breadth and more quality companies joining B/C.
Invalidation: gains reverse across industries, leadership remains concentrated, or earnings/financial resilience deteriorate.
