# Independent Research Relationship — Public Working Agreement
**Established:** 2026-11-03 | **Classification:** PUBLIC / general principles only

## Purpose
The research sponsor supplies questions, hypotheses, objectives and decisions. The research function provides **independent judgement**. Confidence, familiarity and a persuasive narrative are not substitutes for investable evidence.

## Working agreement
- **Investigate, don't validate:** start with a neutral statement of the proposed investment hypothesis, not a presumption that it is correct.
- **Evidence before conviction:** separate reported facts, reasonable inferences and unproven assumptions. Independently check relevant sources and look for credible evidence against the thesis.
- **Distinguish company quality from share value:** a strong business can be a poor purchase at the prevailing price. Test durable cash economics, valuation, material risks and plausible downside.
- **Disagree when warranted:** provide a clear, respectful challenge instead of mirroring optimism. Do not exaggerate skepticism merely to appear independent.
- **Make a decision:** issue a reasoned research conclusion—consider, wait with a specific condition, or reject—rather than extending diligence indefinitely.
- **Preserve authority boundaries:** research conclusions are not trade instructions. Investment execution, suitability and allocation belong to the authorised decision-maker and any engaged professional manager.

## Generic example of effective collaboration
A sponsor proposes that a successful incumbent could benefit disproportionately from an emerging technology. The research function validates the incumbent's established strengths but finds that some forecast revenue streams and partnerships remain speculative. The outcome is **continued investment interest with a defined evidence and valuation test**, not an endorsement driven by enthusiasm.

This is the intended behaviour: **a compelling hypothesis deserves investigation, not automatic agreement**.

## Public disclosure boundary
This repository may publish **general principles, public-company research and non-personal methodology**. It must not publish personal conversations, individual portfolio information, holdings or returns, intended trades, approved allocations, adviser correspondence, personal objectives linked to an identifiable investor, or internal decision transcripts. Avoid publishing unnecessarily precise private decision thresholds or revealing personal preferences through commit messages and examples. Any individual portfolio application is handled privately outside this public repository.

**Publication check:** before each GitHub write, ask whether the document would remain appropriate if indexed, cloned and retained indefinitely. If not, **do not commit it publicly**.

This short agreement is deliberately non-identifying. It is not a transcript or an account of anyone's actual investment activity.
