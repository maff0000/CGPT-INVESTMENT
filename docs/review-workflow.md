# Daily and weekly reviews
All journal dates use Europe/London. Preserve the market observation timezone and distinguish an intraday snapshot from a completed session. These procedures do not create scheduled automations.

## Daily: event and change review
1. Read the previous review and outstanding triggers.
2. Check company IR announcements and material filings for active candidates. Refresh earnings gates, especially imminent and recently reported names.
3. Record new information, source timestamps and whether it changes quality, valuation, exposure or thesis.
4. Inspect meaningful price/volume and relative-strength changes at a common cutoff. Avoid interpreting an incomplete session as a closing signal.
5. Assign advance research / wait / reject / revisit, with reason and next check. “No material change” is a valid result.
6. Append corrections and update the latest watchlist with links to prior snapshots; never erase a rejection.

Use [daily template](../templates/daily-review.md).

## Weekly: complete research cycle
1. Rank sector and industry strength over 1 week, 1 month and 3 months against a comparable S&P 500 series; check breadth.
2. Reconfirm one emerging and two established sectors or document replacements.
3. Run v2 A/B/C per sector. Record exact settings, timestamp, deduplicated candidates and near-misses.
4. Aim for 3–5 names per lane and roughly 10–15 reviews overall. The earlier broad discovery stage allowed 10–20 leads; neither range is a purchase quota.
5. Verify 3–5 years of quality evidence, recent events and valuation. Inspect economic exposure overlap.
6. Refresh earnings gates and distinguish research readiness from entry-review readiness.
7. Publish dated sector assessment, watchlist, decisions and a review of prior hypotheses.
8. Compare outcomes with the predeclared benchmark and method version. Update CHANGELOG for material changes.

Use [weekly template](../templates/weekly-review.md). Initial deep-dive priorities remain ZWS, GMED and MDLZ; timing gates still apply.

## Four-week process review
After four completed weekly cycles, assess usefulness, exclusions, data quality, repeatability and review burden. Do not infer investment efficacy from a tiny sample. Log changes prospectively and retain the previous version. Transfer to an application only after fields, statuses and evidence requirements are sufficiently stable.
