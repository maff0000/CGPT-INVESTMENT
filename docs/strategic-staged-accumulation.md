# Strategic equity — staged accumulation framework
**Policy version:** ACCUMULATION-001 | **Research date:** 2026-11-03 (Europe/London) | **Scope:** generic public investment research, **not a standing order**.

## Objective
Define how to move from **STRATEGIC COMPOUNDER / attractive ownership thesis** to a **bounded, sensible accumulation decision** without requiring perfect market timing, averaging down mechanically or putting defensive capital reserves at risk. Long-horizon compounding is the aim; no strategy guarantees returns or prevents a loss.

The candidate business is evaluated independently of existing cost basis. Prior loss/gain is not a reason to buy, sell, or alter the business valuation. The investor/adviser privately decides capital available and portfolio suitability; **no holdings, personal wealth, transactions, target weights assigned to any individual, tax status or custodian details belong in this public repository.**

## Relationship to the market-wide opportunity reserve
This is a **company-specific illustrative staging tool**, not a mandate to spend long-dated reserve capital or to keep all gilts forever. An independent [opportunity reserve policy](opportunity-reserve-deployment.md) governs how eligible maturity/liquid capital can fund equity accumulation **over time or during broad market dislocations**. Both plans draw on a single authorised risk budget, so a tranche of a strategic name reduces capital left for a future correction. **No price-fall tranche is automatic**: prolonged bear markets can continue repricing businesses while their earnings estimates deteriorate. Existing 35/30/35 examples are optional, not mandatory or personal portfolio instructions.

## Step 1 — risk and concentration permission BEFORE staging
- Review portfolio-equity allocation and **total look-through exposure** (direct shares + index funds + megacap/AI/highly correlated securities), alongside the wider liquidity and liability plan. The investor and professional adviser choose the final maximum.
- As an illustrative discussion starting point only, a single-name limit **around 10–15% of a distinct managed equity sleeve** may be considered, with a separate total-portfolio cap. **This is not a universal or authorised allocation target** and may be too large for a risk-averse portfolio.
- Only budget new purchases **up to available headroom**. If present exposure already meets the limit, **do not add**, even if market price looks cheaper.
- Financial adviser controls appropriate currency, fees, execution and liquidity arrangements; do not use essential-reserve or property-related capital without deliberate separate approval.

## Owner conviction vs illustrative tranche percentages
The **35/30/35** percentage split below is an example only. A pre-approved investor may choose a **larger initial conviction tranche** for a deeply researched strategic compounder at a sensible valuation, while leaving later tranches entirely optional. Any initial increase **must** be within the independently selected maximum stock weight, liquidity reserve and loss tolerance; bigger first tranches imply proportionately less room for later buying. The size of a prior paper loss must not be a reason to increase ownership. See [GOOGL-relative-conviction note](../research/decisions/2026-11-03-alphabet-relative-conviction-entry.md). This public repository records no private quantities or orders.

## Step 2 — use three conditional tranches, not an unconditional averaging-down ladder
For *any adviser-approved incremental budget* (distinct from current holdings), illustrative split:

| Tranche | % of incremental authorised budget | Trigger | Hard condition |
|---|---:|---|---|
| A — Initial conviction | **35%** | Dated price/valuation attractive and post-earnings gate clear | No new purchase if limit or risk cap exceeded, unresolved event, thesis broken or price outside approved range |
| B — Valuation improvement | **30%** | A **10–15% decline** from the dated reference price (e.g., if $280, review ~$238–252), *or a separately justified cheaper valuation* | Decline is **review trigger**, not automatic bid; compare new evidence and company fundamentals |
| C — Evidence | **35%** | Following next quarterly results **and two complete post-release trading sessions**, if cash earnings, spending and long-term economics support the thesis | May buy at a higher price if fair value has risen; may remain unused if valuation/risk no longer compelling |

- Revisit unfilled tranches after **8–12 weeks**, and ordinarily close or re-authorise the plan within about **six months**; do not automatically chase on a rally or leave stale standing orders.
- **Seven exchange trading sessions before published earnings, announcement date, and first two complete sessions after release:** no new entry/addition under the established [earnings policy](earnings-risk-gates.md). Independently verify upcoming company events and any conflicts before each proposed purchase.
- Set a **price limit and order validity** with the adviser per tranche rather than a market-order instruction by this research journal.

## Step 3 — Alphabet-specific investment economics to revalidate
[Alphabet Q3 2026 SEC earnings exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000080/googexhibit991q32026.htm), [earnings call transcript](https://abc.xyz/investor/events/event-details/2026/2026-Q3-Earnings-Call-2026-Ujou16CTw9/default.aspx), reported 2026-10-28:
- Consolidated revenue **+25% to $127.6bn**; Search & other advertising **+12%**; Cloud **+103% to $30.7bn** with meaningful TPU system hardware contribution. Cloud growth is not necessarily the same margin/mix as software services growth.
- Management projected **$195–205bn FY2026 capex** and a material increase in 2027; Q3 operating cash flow **$42.3bn**, capex **$51.4bn**, and FCF **negative $9.1bn**. Exceptional cloud growth must ultimately earn an attractive return on invested capital **after depreciation and operating costs**.
- Monitor search advertising economics, AI serving cost per query, Cloud non-TPU service margins/backlog conversion, capex efficiency, balance-sheet commitments, regulatory remedies, share-class/FX and portfolio overlap with other AI megacaps.
- The shares traded around **$280 on 2026-11-03**, but that is a **historical indicative price**, not current quote or a calculated intrinsic value. [Reference historic share prices](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/GOOGL/history/). The $238–252 band is a mathematical 10–15% pullback illustration **only**, not a preapproved buy range.

## Invalidation — stop additional purchases if
1. Search advertising monetisation suffers sustained structural deterioration not offset by other profitable lines.
2. Cloud cash profit/return on incremental capital does not support infrastructure commitments, or capex/depreciation persistently outruns incremental earnings power.
3. Major regulatory or competitive changes impair the expected economic franchise.
4. Position/sector/FX concentration breaches the approved limits or risk preferences, or the investor's liquidity requirements change.
5. Pricing exceeds a refreshed bear/base/bull long-horizon valuation enough to remove acceptable upside.

## Portfolio-risk arithmetic for decision quality
Risk-budget a **severe 40–50% drawdown** and also recognize the stock can lose more than that; these are stress scenarios, **not price forecasts**. The drawdown is applied to the **whole position after proposed purchases**, not only the incremental tranche. Consider portfolio drawdown, not just reduced average book cost.

The research journal does not know or disclose the investor's position. No purchase is implied or approved. Any actual instructions are to be transmitted privately by the investor to the licensed investment manager.
