# Decision-oriented investment research — adopted 2026-11-03

## Objective
Identify **investable opportunities now**, while maintaining a dated pipeline of **future opportunities**. Research throughput is not success. Each regular review should answer **BUY (starter/full conditional), WAIT, or REJECT** for the highest-priority names, with clear evidence and an explicit reason for waiting where applicable. A missed opportunity is a meaningful outcome, not a permanent excuse to investigate.

This is a public **decision-research framework**, not a brokerage interface or a disclosure of anyone's capital, orders, holdings or financial position. The author retains the final decision; no automatic trades occur.

## Two queues
| Queue | Admission | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **NOW — decision desk** | Verified results and event status, current quote, credible baseline valuation/risk, understandable business, reasonable upside/downside | **BUY STARTER**, **BUY FULL** (only where justified), **WAIT** (explicit price/event/date), or **REJECT**, with dated and bounded rationale |
| **LATER — watchlist** | Quality candidate but price, earnings window, evidence or momentum insufficient | Exact actionable promotion trigger; review date; thesis invalidation |

A stock can be excellent and be a WAIT. Conversely, demanding perfect certainty before ANY small starter tranche is not the goal.

## Minimum viable investment decision, not open-ended research
An immediate-review memo must include:
1. **Current price, market timestamp and source**; no misleading market-close claim for intraday prices.
2. **What we own:** business, economic drivers and material company events/risks.
3. **Why now:** latest reported earnings, change in expectations, valuation or momentum.
4. **Valuation:** reasonable downside/base/upside scenarios with assumptions, not only an analyst target or a single forward P/E. Indicate what could impair capital.
5. **Earnings/event status:** 7 sessions before / 2 complete after, company-verified; missing or conflicting event means no entry review.
6. **Action:** BUY STARTER / BUY FULL / WAIT / REJECT; price/limit condition, max tolerable thesis risk, objective invalidation and next review trigger.
7. **Position risk:** define sizing from **the designated capital allocation and credible loss scenario**, not from excitement; respect diversification and correlation across companies. Record only *public methodology*, never personal account amounts in this repository.
8. **Explicit uncertainties:** unresolved facts cannot be silently treated as passes. However, only material unknowns should hold a starter decision: don't demand a perfect multiyear model when a basic stress case is defensible.

"BUY" denotes a **research opinion conditional on the recorded prices and controls**, never an instruction for a broker to execute without a separate investor decision. Never manufacture execution prices, fill probabilities or liquidity.

## Measured risk, not gambling
- Buying at a fair but imperfect price can be sensible if the business is sound, downside is quantified, the position is appropriately small and there is a defined thesis invalidation.
- A **starter tranche** is for bounded uncertainty: add only on independently improving evidence or more attractive valuation, **not** simply because the quote has fallen.
- A lower P/E or depressed share price alone is insufficient. A rising stock close to its high is not automatically disqualified, but its prospective risk/reward must still be compelling.
- An earnings blackout remains a hard gate. A company with a named other material event may be traded only once that risk is explicitly considered; no automatic override of genuinely unresolved event risk.
- Each cycle should contain **at least one concise immediate-investment decision memo** even if final verdict is WAIT or REJECT. Avoid endless accumulation of candidates and scoring.
- At the end of each cycle publish the NOW rank, LATER rank, change since previous review and the smallest piece of evidence that would reverse each WAIT.

## Relationship to three-sector discovery
Keep **one emerging sector + two established rotation sectors** as a *discovery* funnel. **Do not require every actual purchase to come from each lane.** Cross-sector opportunity, portfolio concentration and risk-adjusted valuation determine decisions. This change affects action prioritisation, not Finviz v2 screening or the seven-plus-two earnings rule.
