# ALL-SECTOR-001 — Expand investment discovery to all remaining sectors
**Decision:** 2026-11-03, Europe/London. **Status: adopted research operating mandate.**

## Why
The founding process focused on **Industrials** (emerging rotation), **Healthcare**, and **Consumer Defensive/Staples** (established rotation). Those remain active, but their dominance would neglect valuable companies elsewhere, especially Technology during an AI-related selloff. A lower sector price trend **is not a veto** when business fundamentals and valuation suggest asymmetric upside. Yet technology fear is not independently a buy signal.

## What changes
- Add a **parallel, weekly ALL-SECTOR opportunity search** covering all **eight other GICS sectors**: Technology, Communication Services, Financials, Consumer Discretionary, Energy, Materials, Real Estate, Utilities.
- Deliver a target **minimum seven distinct, substantiated potential investments per week**, **separate** from the three-sector rotation exercise. An investment candidate is not an instruction to buy.
- The seven must be real, named, liquid listed businesses with a short **why-own/why-now**, priced observation with market time, a fit-for-business valuation benchmark, latest company evidence, next event/earnings state, risk and a **BUY STARTER / WAIT with trigger / REJECT** preliminary view.
- If fewer than seven meet the evidence bar, **expand the search and disclose the verified shortfall** rather than fabricate a seventh stock or relabel an unreviewed ticker. This is an accountability floor, **not permission to lower investment standards**.
- Existing names can requalify in subsequent weeks only with fresh closing-price and event checks and a materially refreshed case. Avoid eight stale repeats presented as new insight. Maintain unique symbols and document promotions, rejections and price changes.
- Seek opportunities in **at least four distinct remaining sectors** every week when enough evidence qualifies; no arbitrary sector quota or insistence on a fixed number of AI stocks. Include non-AI control cases so AI narratives do not dominate.

## AI second-phase lens
1. **Agents' infrastructure:** search/retrieval, secure network access, workloads, observability, identity and permission tooling. Find **revenue conversion**, not only APIs/MCP product availability.
2. **Agentic security:** zero trust identity, data protection, DLP, policy enforcement, threat visibility; look for paid adoption, net retention, margin and cash, not an AI marketing label.
3. **AI-enhanced incumbent:** businesses using AI to improve yield, unit costs, productivity, customer experience, fraud prevention or distribution. Demand evidence of margin or volume improvements **net of deployment and inference costs**.
4. **AI complements:** networks, payments, energy and interconnection that grow as AI demand matures, including second-order beneficiaries.
5. **Dissonance/mean reversion:** quality franchises sold down with their sector although their growth and unit economics are stable or improving.
6. **Non-AI resilience:** don't force every company to benefit from AI; inflation-resilient cash economics, real assets and regulated capacity can still qualify.

**Example: NET Cloudflare** provides AI Search/MCP over content indexed by customer-owned sources; private access requires correct Cloudflare Access configuration. It does not indiscriminately grant a web-browsing agent access to all third-party websites or private content. Verify monetisation and huge valuation premium. **ZS Zscaler** secures agent identity/data and public/private application connectivity; its investor presentations describe ambition, not yet attributable agent-security revenue.

## Screening and promotion
Use QARP v2 as **Discovery Track Q** (unchanged). Add **Innovation/Compounder Track I** for exceptional franchises failing hard v2 multiples or ROIC thresholds. Every Track I exception must explicitly show failed filters and **business proof**, growth-adjusted valuation, share-based compensation/dilution, FCF conversion, capital intensity and bear/base/bull downside.
For **utilities**, focus on regulatory allowed returns, capital employed and financing; for **REITs**, use diluted AFFO/share and debt/capex instead of GAAP P/E; for **energy**, cycle-normalised commodity economics; for **financials/payments**, volume yield and regulation; for **software**, recurring revenues, retention, FCF *after share dilution*, and forward sales multiples if GAAP earnings unavailable.

## Two different clocks
- **Weekly broad discovery:** produce seven or more **decision-worthy investment leads** with price, evidence and catalyst. At least **two** should receive a concise near-term entry assessment if the evidence allows, but no minimum buy count.
- **Daily watch/event review:** track reported earnings, news, clear trigger moves and relative strength. Use the existing **seven sessions pre earnings / two complete sessions post earnings** rule with company-source checks. No trade automation is authorised.

## Outcomes
Measure subsequent price-only/total returns and changes in conviction from a **recorded, reproducible baseline**, compared to the right benchmark and horizon. Record outright rejections and near-misses to limit survivorship bias. Do not confuse **weekly idea output** with portfolio turnover, investment success or backtested alpha.
