# OPPORTUNITY-RESERVE-001 — Gilts/liquidity as capital-deployment reserves
**Recorded:** 2026-11-03, Europe/London. **Research doctrine; no individual holdings or personal balances.**

## Purpose
High-quality short-duration gilts, maturities and liquid reserves may be held primarily as **dry powder for future equity purchases**, **not automatically as a permanent defensive target**. The aim is to build exposure over time, including into attractive market-wide dislocations, while avoiding the classic mistake of mechanically "laddering down" into a prolonged bear market.

A maturity **gilt ladder** manages when capital is available; it is **not** a stock-price averaging-down ladder. Some gilts may fluctuate in market value before maturity (especially longer duration); choose liquidity, maturity and credit/currency profile with a licensed adviser, recognising capital and reinvestment risks.

## Two complementary deployment programmes
1. **Strategic long-term ownership:** consider phased positions in durable franchises whenever valuation and position-risk justify it; do not wait forever for an index crash if a sufficiently attractive business is already available. Use **only an individually authorised allocation** and the company's earnings/valuation gates.
2. **Market dislocation reserve:** retain a **separately authorised reserve** for material equity sell-offs or durable earnings-driven dislocations. Drawdown thresholds (e.g. a broad index down 10%, 20%, 30% from its high) are **alerts for investigation**, not orders. An initial decline may precede much further downside.

These programmes share an overall capital/risk limit. A share bought through the strategic programme consumes headroom that cannot also be counted as future crash liquidity.

## How to avoid an uncontrolled falling-market purchase ladder
**Four gates must be reviewed before each tranche:**
- **Macro / market:** distinguish temporary risk-premium repricing from systemic liquidity stress, recession-driven earnings downgrade, or structurally higher rates. Check breadth, volatility/credit conditions and whether selling pressure shows evidence of stabilisation; no magic moving average or bottom-catching requirement.
- **Company quality:** re-estimate forward **cash flows, leverage, dilution, profitability and earnings revisions** under weaker demand; maintain a genuine long-term moat. Apparent low P/E can be a peak earnings or collapsing-E trap.
- **Valuation:** require a defensible margin of safety using **updated** bear/base/bull economic scenarios, not just price down X%; do not buy more automatically because it is lower.
- **Portfolio permission:** respect remaining committed liquidity, foreseeable liabilities, concentration, correlation, equity allocation and drawdown tolerance. Define maximum tranche and **stop conditions** in advance, and record each proposed tranche as a new decision.

## Illustrative market regimes (triggers, NOT allocations)
| Regime | Research response | Possible action if all four gates clear |
|---|---|---|
| Normal market / selective stock weakness | Evaluate strategic owners on company evidence and valuation | Limited, deliberate starter/accumulation only |
| Broad index drawdown around 10% | Intensify valuation refresh, distinguish quality from falling earnings | Consider small measured tranche; keep substantial dry powder |
| Drawdown around 20% | Stress-test recession earnings, financing and sector contagion | Consider selectively larger tranche if stabilisation and return-to-risk become compelling |
| Drawdown 30%+ / systemic stress | Preserve operational liquidity; investigate balance sheets, spreads, solvency, forced selling and revised intrinsic value | Deploy only under clearly bounded risk and evidence; **no mandatory purchase percentage**, even at extreme discounts |
| Recovery with thesis confirmation | Test higher prices vs revised fair value and economic recovery | Deploy some reserve if justified; do not demand the exact low |

Neither percentage declines nor trend reversal alone guarantee safety. A deployment programme must be **patient about timing and decisive when its evidence gates are met**, without exhausting liquidity early or needing to time the absolute bottom.

## Safeguards
- Reserve for obligations and essential spending comes **before** speculative or growth-equity allocations.
- Plan equity concentration at **portfolio level**, including look-through AI/mega-cap exposure and correlated risk.
- Avoid recording actual holdings, money amounts, family/private obligations, account values, adviser instructions or target personal allocations in this public research repository.
